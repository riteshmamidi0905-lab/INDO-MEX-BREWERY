## Option descriptions
- **A. East Family District (Adventure Court beyond east lawn; Game Hall in eastern restaurant/events zone beside the stage; Outlet beside entrance)**
- **B. South-East Destination Wing (new family/game wing near arrival, separate from the brewery)**
- **C. Split Experience (Adventure Court east of Shade Court; Game Hall deeper inside restaurant/events block; Outlet at entrance/exit)**
- **C+. Split Experience, refined (Game Hall at the east end of the restaurant block behind a double-wall buffer from the stage, glazed face onto the Adventure Court; Outlet in the first bays of the south arcade with its own door)**

## Reading the scores
- **A** puts the Game Hall beside the stage and events room: two loud uses share a wall; noise is the killer.
- **B** is the best for retail and noise isolation but puts children near parking and arrival traffic and splits parents from the brewery experience (worst sightlines and safety).
- **C** is strong but the Game Hall's position 'deeper inside the block' competes with the stage at the court's NE corner and the kitchen.
- **C+** keeps C's logic and fixes the noise problem: the Game Hall sits at the far **east end** of the restaurant block (an extension ~80 ft further east), separated from the stage by the stage's thick back wall plus an acoustic vestibule; its glazed south face looks across the Adventure Court so parents see in and kids see out; the Outlet takes the first two bays of the south arcade beside the entrance with its own exterior door.

## RECOMMENDATION: **Option C, refined (C+)** (56/65), versus C 53, A 47, B 42.
