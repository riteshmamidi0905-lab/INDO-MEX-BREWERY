# Compact Configuration: Functional Program (conceptual; Sprint 3)

**Level:** concept planning only. **No construction precision, code review, MEP or structural validation exists.** ARC-01-D and `visuals/LOCKED-SCENE-DNA.md` remain geometry authority; `architecture/MASTER-PLAN-PHASE2.md` holds areas and phasing. Every row is labelled **KNOWN** (from locked/approved project documents), **ASSUMED** (our planning assumption), or **PRO** (needs architect/engineer/code review). Purpose: give an architect a clear functional brief and expose what must be decided.

## 1. What "Compact" means
Phase A core: Tank Hall (~44×60 ft footprint, KNOWN canon) with brewhouse and a production room; restaurant block (~5,300 sf, ASSUMED/Compact); Shade Court (~7,700 sf with arcades, KNOWN canon); entrance recess and lobby/promenade; compact Outlet (~500 sf, ASSUMED); service yard on the north; surface parking. Family facilities (Adventure Court, Game Hall) are **later** with provisions (SP-1); they are not in this program.

## 4. Kitchen-brewery interface
KNOWN: kitchen is between hall and court; hot-side vent and kitchen exhaust must be separate and downwind of the court. ASSUMED: shared walk-in is **not** assumed; spent grain and beer-brine use (Hefe-Brined Wings) need a clean handoff route and food-safety review. PRO: hoods, make-up air, combustion air, fire separation, and the effect of the tandoor/oven on both kitchen and hall air balance.

## 5. Utility dependencies (list only; sizing PRO)
Electrical (brewing + kitchen), natural gas or propane, steam/boiler, glycol loop, CO₂ bulk storage with detection, water treatment and hot water, compressed air, trade-waste pretreatment and grease interceptor, hoods and make-up air, HVAC, fire protection, stormwater/water quality, data and audio. Dependencies in order: vessel size (G-6) → utilities → hall height/mezzanine decisions → architect scope.
