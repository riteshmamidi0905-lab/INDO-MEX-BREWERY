# Cultural Design Research: Principles to Translate

Rule: **translate principles, do not copy forms.** No sacred sites, temples, mosques, or historically sensitive structures as models. Evidence tags: `[V S-xx]`, `[U]` = widely documented but not yet sourced in this repo, `[Gap]` = research needed.

## 1. Courtyards: what the evidence says
- Measured courtyard studies show strong passive effects: in Seville, shading + misting kept courtyards up to ~10-11.7 °C below reference outdoor conditions `[V S-50b]`; a tropical courtyard showed attenuation peaks of ~5 °C `[V S-50b summary]`; building orientation and ivan integration improved comfort in simulations `[V S-50b]`.
- For **Indian havelis specifically**, the search found only broad reviews (e.g., a hot-dry India review looking at courtyards and jaali walls, IRJET `[V S-50c, C-grade]`) and no measured haveli data `[Gap]`.
- A key caution: Seville is dry; **Austin is humid-subtropical** and misting is less effective as humidity rises `[U]`. Test with local climate data (NOAA) and a mechanical/environmental consultant `[PRO]`.

## 2. Indian principles `[U unless marked]`
| Element | Principle | Translation for Austin |
|---|---|---|
| Chowk/angan (courtyard) | Open center draws air and light, cools at night, organizes rooms | Open or semi-open court between tank hall and dining; stack-effect and cross-ventilation `[PRO]` |
| Haveli | Layered thresholds (street, gate, outer court, inner court) | Layered arrival: roadside, gate, shaded threshold, tank hall, court |
| Jaali | Perforated screen filters sun, allows air, protects privacy | **Functional** shade screens (perforated steel or concrete) on west/south edges; pattern derived from the tank plan (rings), *not* a stock lattice |
| Stepwell (baoli) | Descending space, cooler microclimate, water | Sunken, shaded seating pit near the rill (only if drainage/ADA permit) |
| Material craft | Stone, lime plaster, timber, metalwork (copper, brass, bell metal) | Limestone, lime wash, timber; metalwork limited to copper kettles and blackened steel |
| Contemporary Indian architecture | Climate-responsive, material-honest modernism (e.g., B. V. Doshi, Charles Correa, Studio Mumbai `[U; verify names/awards]`) | Reference for **approach**, not forms |

## 3. Mexican principles
| Element | Principle | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| Patio/hacienda | Rooms open onto a central court; thick walls; shaded corridors (portales) | Deep arcade on 3 sides of the court |
| Barragán | Walled courtyards as "rooms open to the sky"; massive volumes; light and shadow; vivid color from natural pigments `[V S-51b via search summary]` | Use **one** intense color (marigold) and big plain surfaces; avoid pastiche |
| Legorreta | Bold geometry, colored walls, courtyards, fountains, arcades, integration of architecture and landscape `[V S-51a]` | Water and arcade as structure |
| Talavera | Protected denomination of origin (NOM-132-SCFI-1998; zone around Puebla and Tlaxcala) `[V S-41]` | **Do not claim "Talavera"** for non-qualifying tile; use plain glazed handmade tile |
| Volcanic stone (tezontle, cantera) | Locally quarried, thermal mass, red/black porous stone `[U]` | Terrazzo with tezontle-like aggregate; stone base |
| Black clay (barro negro) | Burnished black ceramic from Oaxaca `[U]` | Matte black stoneware tableware |
| Contemporary Mexican architecture | Material-honest, concrete + courts (Barragán lineage) `[V S-51]`; others `[Gap]` | |

## 4. Texas / Austin
- Industrial sheds, exposed steel, brick, limestone; patios; live oaks; live music `[U]`.
- Brewery architecture precedents show equipment as design feature: Brewpoint, Bedford (2022 CAMRA award; industrial exterior reflecting a working brewhouse, extensive copper) `[V S-39a]`; Emerson's, Dunedin (brewing vessels visible from street and restaurant; design award 2017) `[V S-39b]`; Carlsberg Northampton (stepped glass wall following equipment heights; 1975 award) `[V S-39 search]`; Red Leg, Colorado Springs (arched-roof hangar housing a 20-bbl brewhouse) `[V S-39c]`.
- Austin context: Central Machine Works converted a machine shop `[V S-13/14]`; most Austin breweries are industrial-shed taprooms. **Gap:** none appears to treat the tanks as the architectural centrepiece, per our limited search `[C]`.

## 5. Synthesis: seven shared principles
1. A void at the center (court) organizes everything.
2. Thick, shaded edges (arcade, deep eave).
3. Light is filtered, not flooded.
4. Water and planting for cooling and sound.
5. Honest local material (stone, lime, timber, steel).
6. One strong color on plain surfaces.
7. Craft in small, touchable things (pottery, metal, glass).
