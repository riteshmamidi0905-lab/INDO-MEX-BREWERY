Source quality is uneven; most craft-brewery coverage is 2018-2019 and partly promotional. Anything older than 2023 is marked *dated*. Nothing here is a recipe or branding to copy.

**Lesson `[C]`:** India is a lager market driven by mass brands; premiumization is real; a craft brand's job is to be **different in flavor and story**, not cheaper.

## C. Consumer and style patterns `[C]` (where sourced)
- Wheat-led craft start (Toit's first two beers were wheat) `[V S-23b]`; local ingredient experiments (basmati) `[V S-23c]`; brewpub + food model `[V]`.
- Strong high-ABV lagers dominate mass market `[U]`.

## D. Implications for INDO MEX
1. India's beer-drinkers already pair beer with spiced food; **wheat/witbier/clean lagers** are sensible.
2. Don't build a model that depends on cheap contract production and heavy discounting (the Bira 91 path).
3. We borrow nothing: names, tone, packaging all original. Brand check: avoid "owl", "lion", "91", "bira", "toit"-like words in names.
