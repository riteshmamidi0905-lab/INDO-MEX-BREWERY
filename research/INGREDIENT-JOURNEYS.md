# Ingredient Journeys (for the Crossings Gallery, Chile Wall and the future Ingredient Journey game)

Each journey has a **status** and a **display rule**. Only VERIFIED/SUPPORTED journeys may be drawn. Routes on maps must follow sources, not aesthetics.

| Ingredient | Journey | Status | Display rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chile (Capsicum)** | Domesticated in Mexico (S-59) → Europe (Columbian exchange) → Portuguese trade to Goa and India in the 1500s (S-45) → Indian cuisines; also Africa and Asia generally | Origin VERIFIED; India arrival SUPPORTED | Map with *uncertain-route* dashed lines and a note "routes simplified; dates vary"; no single date |
| **Maize** | Domesticated ~9,000 years ago in southwestern Mexico (S-64) → across the Americas | VERIFIED (origin) | Show origin and a few archaeological sites; **no claim about maize in India until researched** |
| **Wheat and barley** | Old World crops; Harappan wheat/barley evidence (S-63); wheat reached Sonora with Spanish settlers (S-48) | Partly VERIFIED | Two separate stories: Indus and Sonora; do not connect them |
| **Millets** | *Panicum* and *Setaria* millets found at Farmana (S-63) | VERIFIED (India) | India-only panel |
| **Turmeric, ginger** | Harappan evidence at Farmana (S-63) | VERIFIED | India-only |
| **Cacao** | Mesoamerican origin | UNRESOLVED (not yet sourced) | Research first |
| **Mango, tamarind** | To Mexico via galleon? | UNRESOLVED | Hold |
| **Marigold** | Native to Mexico (S-46); route to India unresolved | Origin VERIFIED; route UNRESOLVED | Show as a flower of Mexico; do not assert the India route |
| **Silver** | Mexico/Peru → Manila → China/Asia (S-61) | VERIFIED | Crossing theme; trade not food |
| **Agave/pulque** | Mesoamerican fermented agave | UNRESOLVED | Research first |
| **Rice** | Asian crop; Northeast India rice beers (S-74a); rice to Mexico? | Partly | Keep separate |

## Display design implications
- Maps use thin white lines on dark steel with **no invented arrows**; unsure routes are dashed and flagged.
- Every card carries a "confidence" mark (solid, dashed) so visitors see what is known.
- The game must read from a verified dataset in `research/` and reject unsourced routes.
