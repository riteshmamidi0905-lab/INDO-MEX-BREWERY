# Historical Claims Audit

**Rule (D-25, D-30): never invent history to improve the brand.** Each claim has a status and the wording we may use *today*. Nothing here may be used in signage, menus, packaging or the website until its status is VERIFIED and a second, scholarly source is added. Source IDs refer to `research/SOURCES.md`.

Status key: **VERIFIED** (strong source read) · **SUPPORTED** (credible secondary, needs scholarship) · **UNRESOLVED** (sources conflict or none found) · **NO CONNECTION ESTABLISHED** · **DO NOT USE**.

## E. No connection established (say so)
- **Tandoor ↔ comal:** no historical link; each arose independently as a clay/heated cooking surface.
- **Maize culture ↔ millet culture:** no historical link; they are parallel foundations of food systems.
- **Black pottery (Oaxaca/India), terrazzo, tezontle:** no *verified* shared tradition; design only (D-25).
- **Fermented-drink traditions** (apong, tesgüino, beer): parallel human inventions, not connected.
