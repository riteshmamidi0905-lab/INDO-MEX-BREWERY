## The deeper thesis found in research: "The Crossing"
India and Mexico are not strangers. Five hundred years ago their ingredients began crossing:
- **Chile** reached India from the Americas via Portuguese trade in the 16th century (Goa the likely gateway); before it, heat came from black pepper and long pepper. `[V S-45a,b]` (dates vary)
- **Mango and tamarind** reached Mexico via Asian trade including the Manila galleon; rice and coconut too. `[V S-47a,b; C-grade; direction and origins disputed]`
- **Marigold** (cempasúchil/*Tagetes erecta*) is native to Mexico and Central America, with a Nahuatl name; in India it became *genda*, central to ritual and decoration; the Portuguese role is widely repeated but not firmly sourced. `[V S-46; partial]`
- **Wheat flatbread** met **corn** in Mexico (flour tortilla in Sonora) just as it did in India (roti/naan). `[V S-48]`
- **Punjabi-Mexican families** formed in California's Imperial and Yuba valleys from 1916, a real community history of shared kitchens (food specifics not found). `[V S-36a,b]`
- **Al pastor** shows Mexican cuisine absorbing a migrant technique (Lebanese shawarma). `[V S-37]`

This gives the brand a *true* story: **not "Indian + Mexican", but two food cultures that already trade fire, fruit and flower.** Caution: the dates, directions and individual crossings above must be re-verified with primary food-history sources (e.g., *The Oxford Companion to Food*, Columbian Exchange scholarship) before any published claim. `[PRO/Research]`

## Decisions
- **BUILD:** Kerala × Yucatán; Telangana × Puebla (salan ↔ mole); Goa × Yucatán; Goa × Baja; Punjab × Sonora.
- **SELECTIVE:** Telangana × Jalisco (kept for birria *technique* but no longer the brand's centre), Kerala × Veracruz, Punjab × Jalisco, Mumbai × Mexico City (street dishes only), Rajasthan × Oaxaca (**design only**), Andhra × Oaxaca.
- **PARK:** Chettinad × Veracruz, Gujarat × Puebla (veg program inspiration), Northeast × Oaxaca (needs community consultation).
- **REJECT:** Karnataka × Chiapas (coffee stays a standalone beer ingredient), West Bengal × Veracruz.
- **Change from Phase One:** Rajasthan × Oaxaca demoted from A to design-only; two new pairings (Goa × Yucatán, Telangana × Puebla) enter the top tier on technique grounds.
