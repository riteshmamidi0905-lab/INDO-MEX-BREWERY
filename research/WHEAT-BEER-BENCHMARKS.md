# Wheat Beer Benchmarks

Goal: brewing literacy and sensory benchmarks. **No recipes are reverse-engineered.** Confidence flags are explicit; items from homebrew sources are not authoritative and need confirmation from a licensed head brewer and a yeast supplier.

## 1. Style standards
- **BJCP 10A Weissbier:** OG 1.044-1.057, FG 1.008-1.014, IBU 10-18, SRM 14-23, ABV 4.3-5.6%; banana and clove expected low to moderately strong; bubblegum is a fault; high carbonation, fluffy mouthfeel. `[V S-06]`
- **BJCP 10B Dunkles Weissbier:** darker variant. `[V S-06]`
- **Brewers Association:** South German-style hefeweizen uses at least 50% malted wheat and is very highly carbonated. `[V S-07]`

## 3. Technique knowledge (confidence in brackets)
- **Clove (4-vinylguaiacol):** a ferulic-acid rest around 43-45 °C (110-113 °F) for ~15 min releases ferulic acid, which POF+ yeast converts; clove highest after attenuation limit. `[Medium: S-44a, homebrew-grade]`
- **Banana (isoamyl acetate):** cooler fermentation limits it; warmer/under-pitched pushes it; stated ranges 62-66 °F (17-19 °C) in sources, and these disagree; **yeast strain matters more than any setting**. `[Medium-low: S-44a,b]`
- **Pitch rate:** one recipe cites ~0.5 M cells/ml/°P; no consensus. `[Low]`
- **Carbonation:** ~3.0 volumes CO₂ cited in recipes; style is "highly carbonated". `[Medium]`
- **Open fermentation, decoction, wheat percentage beyond 50%:** `[Unresolved]` get from Eric Warner's *German Wheat Beer* (Brewers Publications) or Weihenstephan technical literature.
- **Glass and pour:** tall vase glass leaves room for head; pour at an angle, swirl yeast sediment for the last pour (hefe). `[U]`

## 4. Belgian witbier (separate tradition)
- Style basics: unmalted wheat + malted barley, sometimes oats, coriander and orange peel. `[V S-42a,b,c]`
- History: the last witbier brewery (Tomsin, Hoegaarden) closed between 1955-1960 depending on source; Pierre Celis revived the style c.1965-66 (De Kluis); sold to Interbrew 1985 after a fire; Hoegaarden later criticized as industrialized; Celis founded Celis Brewery in **Austin in 1992**, later sold to Miller; a new Celis reopened in Austin in 2017 with Celis's daughter Christine. `[V S-42a]` (dates vary)
- **Local resonance:** Austin already has the American origin of the Belgian-style witbier revival. We should not compete as "the witbier place"; we may *collaborate* (Celis) on a spiced witbier with Indian coriander-seed and citrus. `[C]`

## 5. Sensory benchmark approach (see `beer/HEFEWEIZEN-BENCHMARK.md`)
Buy fresh examples of the reference producers, taste blind with a trained panel against our pilot batches, score banana/clove/bread/ester/phenol balance, haze, foam stability, carbonation, finish. Record vs. BJCP.
