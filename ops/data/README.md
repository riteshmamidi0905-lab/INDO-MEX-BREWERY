# ops/data: real observed datasets only
`austin-yelp-observed.csv`: 49 Austin-area business listings returned by the Yelp connector on 2026-10-11 from 9 searches (brewery taproom, brewpub, Indian restaurant, Mexican restaurant, fusion restaurant, beer garden, family friendly brewery patio, Indian Mexican fusion tacos; max 8 results per search). It is a SAMPLE of what Yelp ranked for those queries, NOT a census of Austin. Ratings and review counts are Yelp users' aggregates at the access date. The connector returned no coordinates, hours, review text or photos list (only a cover image URL), so no geospatial or review-theme analysis is possible from it. The connector's `distance_meters` field is not used: its values (1 to 16) are not plausible metres and the unit is unverified. Yelp's own terms for storing/republishing this data are UNVERIFIED: keep it internal, do not publish it on the website, and re-check terms before any wider use. Revenue is never inferred.
