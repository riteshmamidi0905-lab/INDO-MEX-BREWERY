# Supplier packages: how to use

- Packages are in `SUPPLIER-PACKAGES-FOOD.md` (10 packages, same fields).
- NOTHING HAS BEEN SENT. No supplier has been contacted. Draft messages are templates with [brackets] to fill.
- The founder approves each send individually, by supplier. No agent sends anything.
- Record every reply as a new row in `ops/templates/vendor-rfq-comparison.csv` (set `contact_authorized_by_founder`, `date_sent`, `evidence_tier`). Leave price cells blank until a written reply gives one.
- Keep raw replies (emails, PDFs, texts, call notes) untouched in a separate folder; never edit or paraphrase over the original. Summaries go in `notes`.
- One quote is not a market price. Get at least 2-3 quotes per item group before treating any figure as a planning input, and note quantity, date and validity.
- Quantities are test-batch quantities for Sessions 1-5 (not forecasts). Most suppliers will not serve them at wholesale terms; ask about retail or small-lot options.
- Supplier-discovery rows (`ops/data/supplier-discovery.csv`) are T1 search snippets, not vetted vendors. Wording in packages: "potential supplier, not contacted".
- D-54 applies to every package: no beef, no pork, no lard, animal fats, gelatin or animal rennet of bovine source. No halal claim is requested or implied. Mustard oil is excluded (V-72).
- Session numbering follows the order in `food/sessions/sessions.json`: S2 paneer taco, S3 gobi taco, S4 butter chicken quesadilla, S5 goat birria. S1 is Chicken 65 Totopos (sheet 13).
- Not professional review: a licensed chef/food-safety professional and the licensing authority own sourcing and permit decisions.
