## Main LinkedIn caption

What happens when my AI specialists start working together?

For NASHA AI, I wanted to move beyond twelve impressive-sounding agent names and build software that can actually exchange structured results.

Here's one example from my synthetic inventory workflow:

• **VECTRA** identifies a stock shortfall.
• **QUANTIX** models the financial scenario.
• **NEXORA** compares synthetic supplier quotes and drafts a recommendation.
• **AEGIS** checks the proposal against business rules.
• **VERITAS** evaluates the deterministic checks.
• **SYNAPSE** helps coordinate the specialist handoffs.

These are callable Python services with typed results, tests, and correlation-based audit records—not twelve separate LLMs.

The important boundary: a procurement recommendation is not permission to purchase. I'm integrating THE WEIR, my independent MCP security gateway, to enforce authorization and human approval. That end-to-end integration is still being verified.

NASHA itself is an Indo-Mexican brewpub concept. The scenarios, inventory, and supplier quotes are synthetic.

I built this to explore how AI engineering, business analytics, workflow design, and security come together around a practical business problem.
