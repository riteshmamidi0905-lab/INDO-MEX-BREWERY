# 08. Assumptions register

**SYNTHETIC / ASSUMPTION-DRIVEN.** NASHA has no operation, customers, suppliers, staff or sales. Everything below is an assumption or a limit of the evidence. Nothing is validated. Each row has an owner (a role that would have to check it), a way to falsify it, and the effect on the decision. Money and volume values are PLACEHOLDERS used only by `lab/product_case/cost_model.py`; none is an estimate of anything real.

Status for every row at the time of writing: **UNVALIDATED**.

## Sensitivity: which assumptions can reverse the recommendation

Ranked by how much of the decision they carry (from `lab/product_case/results/cost_model.json`, 20,000 seeded draws):

1. **A-10 (rule-gap rate g)** and **A-11 (what a model could catch)**. Together they decide whether any AI component can pay off. Unmeasured.
2. **A-07 (cost of a miss)** and **A-06 (actionable share)**. Decide whether false alarms or misses dominate.
3. **A-04 (local models only)**. Bounds which models the "do not use an LLM planner" statement covers.
4. **A-13 (suite proxy quality)**. Bounds how much weight any synthetic number carries.

Everything else changes magnitudes, not rankings.
