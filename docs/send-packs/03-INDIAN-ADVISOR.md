# Pack 3: Indian culinary advisor
**Context.** A concept-stage project is developing five dishes that combine Indian and Mexican cooking for a future Austin restaurant. Nothing has been cooked for the public or published. We ask you to correct us.
**What it is.** Five dishes, one page each, with the Indian reference, what we change and why, what we believe and what we are unsure of.
**What it is NOT.** Not a request for endorsement; not marketing; not a claim of authenticity or of shared Indian-Mexican history. We do not ask "is it authentic?"
**Questions (short answers or "I do not know" are useful).** Is the Indian component still recognisable in each dish? Is the other component recognisable? Is our terminology accurate (names to check: Chicken 65 (Chennai-associated; origin contested), paneer tikka, gobi, butter chicken, the tandoor and the dum-style technique used for goat)? Does any origin or history claim mislead? Does any technique create a false cultural claim? Does anything feel stereotyped, flattened or disrespectful? What should be described as modern fusion rather than tradition? What visual cue or ingredient matters? Anything we must not say? (We keep the Indian and Mexican reviews independent.)
**Please send back.** The one-page response form, a tick (OK / FIX / REMOVE / UNSURE) and a line per dish. Say whether your reply is cultural advice only. Nothing will carry your name without your written agreement.
