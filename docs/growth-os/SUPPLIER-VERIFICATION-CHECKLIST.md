# Supplier verification checklist (12 research leads)

**Status of every lead below: unverified.** No official page has been opened. This checklist tells a person exactly what to inspect. A lead may be marked `verified_public` only when a person has opened the official page, and the database then also requires a website URL, a source URL and a verification date. Until then every record stays `lead_unverified` and shows its evidence tier. No supplier is to be contacted: verification is reading public pages only.

## Fields to capture for every lead (from the primary page, never from a directory)

1. **Legal name**: as shown in the site footer, terms or About page; cross-check against the Texas Secretary of State or Comptroller entity search (government record).
2. **Official website**: the exact domain, confirmed by an HTTPS page that names the same business. A search-result snippet is not enough.
3. **Category**: what the business says it sells, in its own words (quote it).
4. **Service area**: where it says it delivers or serves; keep conflicting statements as conflicts.
5. **Public contact channel**: record the *kind* and the page URL (for example 'contact form at <url>'). Do not store e-mail addresses or phone numbers; the database rejects them.
6. **Published product information and prices**: record only what is public. If prices need an account, record 'no public prices (account required)'. Never infer or estimate a price.
7. **Source URL, access mode and date seen**, with a one-line statement of what the page says. Choose access `page_read` only if the page was actually opened.
8. **Limitations**: login walls, stale pages, conflicting sources, entity ambiguity.

Pass rule: items 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7 must come from primary pages; item 6 may be 'none published'. If any of these cannot be established, the lead stays unverified and the gap is written down.

## Recording a result

For each lead a verifier records: date, the exact URLs opened, and for each field the quoted text. Then, in the Suppliers screen, add the source with access mode 'I opened and read the page'. Moving a record to `verified_public` is a separate, deliberate step that needs website, source and date; none of the 12 leads has had it taken, and none should be moved on the strength of this checklist alone.
