# NASHA staffing architecture (conceptual)

**Status: CONCEPT.** No salaries, wages, headcounts or costs are claimed; `ops/finance/staffing-roles.csv` is still blank and is not touched. Data: `docs/company/data/staffing-stages.csv` (75 rows: 5 stages x 15 roles; see `staffing-stages-NOTE.md`). Stages align to `validation/MOBILE-MVP-STRATEGY.md` (pop-up, residency) and the flagship. "Trailer" is not a separate staffing stage here; treat it as the pop-up/residency team with a tighter menu.

## Principles
1. **Roles, not people.** One person may hold several roles at small stages; the CSV says which combinations are unsafe.
2. **Raw-poultry/goat handlers do not also do allergen-sensitive vegetarian prep** (paneer, gobi) in the same block without full separation; strict-vegetarian claims remain pending verification.
3. **Brewer vs FOH:** brewing (hot liquor, caustic CIP, timing) and guest service/alcohol service should not be one person in one shift.
4. **Alcohol:** pop-up/residency/trailer staff do not sell NASHA alcohol; the licensed host does (V-26, T1). Training or certification needed for anyone serving alcohol: verify with TABC and a Texas alcohol attorney.
5. **Cash and books:** the person who handles cash should not be the only person who reconciles it.
6. **Thermometer and second-person checks** stay with the kitchen lead; the frozen Chicken 65 protocol (D-74) is not altered by staffing choices.

## Who verifies (no claims made)
Food safety: certified food protection manager and handler training requirements, and who must be on site, to VERIFY with the Austin/Travis County authority (DSHS for mobile). Alcohol: server/seller training, permits, minors rules to VERIFY with TABC and a Texas alcohol attorney. Brewer qualifications: employer and regulator decide (federal and state brewing permits: attorney). Payroll, tax and classification: accountant and attorney. Nobody in this file is certified, and no certification is assumed to exist.
