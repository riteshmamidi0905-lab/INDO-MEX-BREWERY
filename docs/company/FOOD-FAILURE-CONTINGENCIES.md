# Food failure contingencies (Core 5)

Status: planning. Nothing has been cooked or tasted. Failure signs and gates come from `food/sessions/sessions.json` and `food/CORE5-FOOD-MATRIX.csv`. Rule of the sessions: change ONE variable per rework; "NONE WINS" is a valid result; gates are never amended after tasting. Decisions to REWORK/HOLD/CUT belong to the founder with a chef; this is a proposal. Chicken 65 Session 1 is frozen (D-74); its gate detail lives in the sheet-13 protocol, not sessions.json, so the Chicken 65 triggers below use the matrix's 10-minute failure gate and are INFERRED.

Definitions. REWORK = another single-variable round. HOLD = stop cooking it, keep it on the list, revisit after a named input arrives. CUT = remove from the Core 5 for v1.

## 1. IF Chicken 65 Totopos fails repeatedly
- **Looks like:** chip goes soft by T+10 (moisture migration), coating slides off or goes gummy, chicken dry, or it reads as "fried chicken on nachos".
- **First move (one variable):** serve chip and chicken separately on the board (construction C) before touching anything else. If it still fails, change the coating or fry temperature, not both.
- **REWORK:** crisp at T+0 but fails only at T+10, or only one construction fails. **HOLD:** all three constructions fail the 10-minute gate across two rounds and the chip and chicken each pass alone. **CUT:** both components fail alone (a recipe problem, not a fusion problem), or the dish can only work with a fryer the plan can't support.
- **What survives:** Chicken 65 as a plain bar snack with curry leaf, and the idea of a corn element (chips) served alongside rather than under it. The mission (Indian flavor in a Mexican format) can live in the taco dishes.

## 2. IF Goat Birria's fusion layer adds no value over plain adobo
- **Looks like:** in the blind taste, control A (plain adobo) scores equal or higher than B (technique layer) or C (yogurt-papaya) on `improves_over_control` (gate 3.5), or B/C read as GENERIC_CURRY.
- **First move:** drop C, keep A vs B only, and move one thing in B (e.g. bhuna time or whole-spice toast) rather than add new spices. Curry-leaf tadka stays out until a cultural advisor weighs in.
- **REWORK:** B scores close to A and the difference is one adjustable technique. **HOLD:** no layer beats control in two rounds but the control itself passes goat identity and yield, so ship plain goat birria as a "Mexican control" only if the founder accepts it breaks the three-yes test, or wait for the advisor. **CUT:** control fails (tough, gamey, greasy consomme, poor yield) or the cooling/reheat process can't be approved by a food-safety professional.
- **What survives:** a goat taco that clearly reads as goat (D-54, no beef), the Indian technique where it demonstrably helps (toasted whole spices, bhuna), and a side element (pickled onion, chutney-style salsa) carrying the India signal without touching the braise.

## 3. IF Gobi cannot hold crisp / read as cauliflower
- **Looks like:** SOGGY by T+5, glaze slides off, or it reads as chicken nuggets (NOT_CAULIFLOWER).
- **First move:** glaze last and less, or sauce on the side, before changing the cooking method. Then compare roast vs batter-fried (the session's actual test variable).
- **REWORK:** one arm fails only one gate (glaze adhesion, crisp at T+10). **HOLD:** both arms are soggy at T+10 but taste and recognition pass; wait for a fryer/oven answer and test a dry-glaze or dusting option. **CUT:** cauliflower can't be recognised in either arm, or the dish can only be served as a plated, not handheld, item.
- **What survives:** a roasted cauliflower with tamarind-guajillo glaze served as a vegetarian plate or bar bite, keeping the vegetarian slot on the menu; the tamarind-chile idea can move to a sauce.

## 4. IF the Quesadilla is too comal-hungry or leaks
- **Looks like:** more than a few minutes per plate on the comal (capacity exercise assumes 18 slot-minutes, unmeasured), queueing at the comal, or sauce escaping at the cut / T+10 (LEAKS, SOGGY).
- **First move:** sauce thickness (A thick vs B moderate, the session's variable). Only then cheese barrier, then chicken size. Pre-fold and finish in oven/pan as a labelled alternative only if the time is the problem.
- **REWORK:** thicker sauce fixes the leak, or comal time is the only gate failing. **HOLD:** it leaks or soaks even when thick, or comal minutes per plate cannot fit service at the measured pace; wait for the kitchen consultant on comal size. **CUT:** reads as plain cheese quesadilla (NOT_BUTTER_CHICKEN) after thickening, or the comal bottleneck would hurt the other taco dishes.
- **What survives:** butter chicken as a taco filling or a dip/side, and roasted poblano rajas as a garnish; the "melted cheese + makhani" idea can return as a smaller format.

## 5. IF paneer cannot get char without a tandoor
- **Looks like:** NO_CHAR, RUBBERY or WEEPING on charbroiler and comal arms; the dish reads as grilled cheese (NOT_TIKKA).
- **First move:** keep heat source as the variable: try the charbroiler/very hot grill and a hot dry comal, keep marinade, cut size and tortilla constant. Then drier marinade (hung curd) or smaller cubes in a separate round. Do NOT simulate a tandoor; record the limitation.
- **REWORK:** char is achieved on one substitute but misses another gate (moisture, tortilla integrity). **HOLD:** no substitute reaches char >= 3.5 and tandoor access is the only open path; wait for the architect/hood answer and a real tandoor trial. **CUT:** char only appears with equipment the site can't have, and tikka identity < 4 without it.
- **What survives:** the paneer taco without tandoor claims (labelled "grilled/charred paneer" with the tikka marinade), or paneer as a seared bite with the tikka spice set; a tandoor stays an option, not a requirement.
