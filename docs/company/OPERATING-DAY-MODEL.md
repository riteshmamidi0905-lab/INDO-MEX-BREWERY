# NASHA conceptual operating day model

**Status: CONCEPT.** NASHA does not exist; nothing has been cooked, brewed, timed or reviewed. No headcounts, times, volumes or costs are claimed (all TBD). This is not a HACCP plan, not a food-safety programme, not alcohol-service guidance. Food safety: verify with the Austin/Travis County health authority (or DSHS for mobile units). Alcohol, minors, to-go: verify with a Texas alcohol attorney / TABC. Inputs (observed in repo): `docs/OPERATIONAL-REQUIREMENTS-CORE5.md` (comal/labour bound is an assumption set, not data; goat cooling; veg vs raw separation), `validation/MOBILE-MVP-STRATEGY.md` (food truck cannot sell alcohol, V-26 T1), `business/FAMILY-EXPERIENCE-OPERATIONS.md` (minors UNRESOLVED). D-54: no beef/pork; no halal claim.

Format tags: **PU** pop-up, **RS** residency, **TR** trailer, **SP** small permanent, **FL** flagship. Brewing/cellar blocks apply only to SP and FL (PU/RS/TR cannot brew or sell NASHA beer; a licensed host or partner handles alcohol, attorney to confirm). Day is a sequence of blocks, not clock times.

## 1. Operating day (conceptual)
| Block | Who (role, not headcount) | What | Collision risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receiving | Chef/sous, brewer (grain, hops, kegs), cellar | Check temp/tags on chicken, goat, paneer; dry goods; grain and CO2 | Brewer deliveries crossing guests; raw protein crossing produce/dairy at the dock; one door for food and grain |
| Morning prep | Prep, cooks | Veg prep (gobi, slaw, onion), paneer marinade, masa/chip prep | Raw chicken and goat handled before vegetarian prep in same area/with same tools |
| Brewing | Head/asst brewer | Mash, boil, transfer, CIP (SP/FL only) | Hot work, wet floors and hoses in shared routes; steam/heat load on kitchen; brewer pulled into FOH |
| Fermentation work | Brewer, cellar | Gravity/temp checks, yeast handling, dry hops | Cold-side access vs walk-in traffic; tank access blocked by deliveries |
| Kitchen prep (hot) | Cooks | Goat braise, sauces, chicken marinade, tortilla/comal setup | Goat cooling needs shallow pans/ice bath/walk-in space at the same time as service stock |
| Pre-service | Whole team | Station set, allergen briefing, glass and keg check, thermometers/sanitiser buckets | Comal and fryer heat-up competing with prep; glass stock not polished; no briefing time |
| Lunch (if applicable) | Reduced line, bar | Family/daytime trade, counter or table | Minors and alcohol adjacency (attorney); thin staff doing prep and service together |
| Happy hour | Bar, servers, line | Beer-led, snack-led (Chicken 65 Totopos) | Beer-glass shortage; fryer chip surge; bar queue blocking the host stand |
| Dinner rush | All FOH, full line | Peak plates and pours | Comal queue (quesadilla); chips at peak (crispness window short); dish pit congestion; patio service distance; keg change mid-service |
| Closing | Line, bar, FOH | Breakdown, label/date, cool leftovers, last pours | Hot goat/consomme cooling at close with no capacity left; tired handwashing discipline |
| Cleaning | Dish, closers, cellar | Kitchen deep-clean, fryer oil, draft line clean, CIP | Chemicals near food/glassware; draft cleaning and kitchen cleaning on one sink |
| Beer cellar | Cellar/brewer | Keg change-out, cold room, CO2 check, line care | Keg movement through dining; cold room shared with food |
| Waste | Dish/closers | Food waste, spent grain, fryer oil, glass, packaging | Spent grain and bins crossing guest or clean routes; odour at patio |
| Inventory | Chef (food), brewer/bar (beer, packaging) | Counts, par, ordering, goat/lamb supply | Counting done by the person who also receives (control); stock-outs of tortillas, glasses |
| Handoff | Closer to opener, GM log | Temps/cooling log, 86 list, equipment faults, keg status | Verbal-only handoff; cooling not completed and not recorded |

## 2. Collision list to design requirements
| # | Collision | Design requirement (what space/process must provide) | Formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Brewer deliveries (grain, kegs, CO2) cross guests | Back-of-house receiving route separate from guest entry; delivery windows outside service; grain and keg staging off guest paths | SP, FL (RS/PU: host's dock, agree route in writing) |
| C2 | Raw chicken/goat crossing vegetarian prep (paneer, gobi) | Physically/time separated raw-protein station and veg station; colour-coded boards/utensils; handwash sink at each; veg-dedicated fryer oil path decision for a professional; allergen board | All; physical separation: SP, FL; time/utensil separation: PU, RS, TR |
| C3 | Keg movement through dining | Keg route from cold room to bar not through seating, or keg moves only outside service; cold room adjacent to bar | SP, FL; RS (host's layout, verify) |
| C4 | Dish pit congestion (plates plus glasses plus pots) | Separate or zoned pot-wash, ware-wash, and glass wash; clean/dirty flow one-way; return path not through the pass | SP, FL; PU/RS/TR: disposable-vs-reusable and commissary wash plan |
| C5 | Chip frying at peak | Fryer sized/located with a pre-fry hold plan; chips staged to order-fire; short fry-to-guest path; measure chip crispness decay (Session 1) | All; patio/take-away path matters at SP, FL |
| C6 | Comal queue (quesadilla, goat tacos, tortilla warm) | Comal bank next to pass, enough positions; assumed bottleneck, to be measured (quesadilla comal minutes); menu limits on small units | All; menu reduction for PU, TR; bank sizing SP, FL |
| C7 | Beer-glass shortage | Glass par plus fast glass wash plus storage near bar; fewer glass shapes (ops preference, unresolved) | RS (host's glassware, verify), SP, FL |
| C8 | Patio service distance | Service station/runner path to patio; chip and hot-item time limit; second bar or satellite decision | SP, FL; TR/PU only if guests eat on site |
| C9 | Goat cooling vs service | Shallow-pan/ice-bath space and walk-in capacity reserved for braise cooling; time/temp log; cooling is not displaced by service stock; professional confirms method and limits | All with goat; capacity: SP, FL; PU/RS: commissary capacity to confirm |
| C10 | Brewing heat, steam, wet floors vs kitchen and guests | Brewhouse zoned from kitchen and dining; drains and floors independent of guest routes; ventilation | SP, FL |
| C11 | Draft line cleaning and CIP chemicals near food | Chemical storage and sink separate from food prep and glass wash; labelled | SP, FL |
| C12 | Spent grain, oil and waste crossing clean routes | Waste route separate from receiving and guest paths; grease and grain handling area | SP, FL; PU/RS/TR: host or commissary waste plan |
| C13 | Minors near alcohol service (family hours) | Hours/zone policy; separation of bar from play/children's area; rules from attorney | SP, FL (RS if host allows minors; verify) |
| C14 | Per the T1 ledger (V-26/V-27, unverified), a mobile or pop-up unit may be unable to hold an alcohol permit; a Texas alcohol attorney confirms. Plan as if the licensed host sells the beer | Alcohol sold only by the licensed host/partner; no beer on NASHA menu board as a sale; attorney reviews pairing and advertising | PU, RS, TR |
| C15 | Closing cooldown, labelling, handoff gaps | Cooling and date-label station, handoff log template, closer-to-opener checklist | All |
| C16 | One person covering brewing and service | Role separation rules (see `STAFFING-ARCHITECTURE.md`); schedule that avoids brewer on floor at peak | SP, FL |
