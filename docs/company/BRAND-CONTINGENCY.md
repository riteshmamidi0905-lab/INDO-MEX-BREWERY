# Brand contingency strategy (D-78 follow-up)
NASHA stays the founder-selected working name. Nothing is renamed, no alternative name is announced, no domain is bought, no logo is made (D-66) and no Figma calls are used. This note exists so that, if an IP attorney advises changing the name, the project loses as little as possible. It draws no legal conclusion: infringement, availability and safety of the name are all undetermined and are for counsel (see `ip/NASHA-TRADEMARK-ATTORNEY-PACKAGE.md`, `docs/company/NASHA-CONFLICT-FACTS.md`).

## 1. What must survive any name
Indo-Mex culinary collaboration; food and beer designed together; warm Austin hospitality; cultural respect; craft brewing; discovery and shared experiences. These live in the Core 5, the cultural pack, the experience standards, the responsible-alcohol principles and the evidence systems, none of which depend on the name.

## 2. Asset split
Full table: `docs/company/data/asset-classes.csv` (18 asset categories, each NAME-DEPENDENT / NAME-INDEPENDENT / REUSABLE SYSTEM / LEGAL REVIEW, with what to do if the name changes). Summary: **name-independent or reusable:** Core 5 food and beer work, protocols, cultural pack, visual direction rules, experience standards, responsible-alcohol principles, decision and evidence systems, brand essence. **Name-dependent:** website copy, domain and handle inventory, brand architecture names, tagline, merchandise/packaging concepts, investor materials. **Legal review needed whatever the name:** AI concept images and the concept website rights, glass design protection, ownership of AI-assisted assets, any tagline.

## 3. Spend discipline while the name is unresolved
No further name-dependent spend or public use until the founder has counsel's view. Track any such spend in `ops/templates/expenses-receipts.csv` (flag Y/N in the name_dependent_spend (Y/N) column and record sunk, refundable or reusable in notes). Categories at risk: domains and hosting; website build; printed menus, labels, packaging, merchandise; signage and any vehicle graphics (blank only); beer labels and tap handles; entity and assumed-name filings; insurance and payment accounts in the name; paid ads; brand and photography agency work; contracts naming the brand; uniforms.

## 3b. Temporary internal identifier
Until the name is resolved, internal and non-public work (cooking sessions, panel, cultural and brewer reviews, supplier enquiries, private tastings) uses the neutral working label **Indo-Mex Brewery & Kitchen (working project label)**, not the NASHA name. See `docs/company/VALIDATION-PATH.md`.

## 4. If counsel recommends a change (process, not a name)
1. Founder reads counsel's written view and decides; record the decision as a new D-entry (old position -> evidence -> new position; do not rewrite D-62/D-78).
2. Use counsel's advice on the existing fallback material (screened candidates in `brand/TRADEMARK-SCREENING.md`; shortlists in `brand/NAMING-UNIVERSE-V2.md`); apply the same screening to any new candidate: counsel search first, then meaning/pronunciation check with cultural advisors, then alcohol-branding check, then domains and handles (nothing is bought before clearance).
3. Replace name strings in the concept website and copy; keep structure. Regenerate name-bearing images only with blank signage and only on founder approval.
4. Keep the essence, Core 5 and experience standards unchanged; they are not the name.
**Criteria any candidate should meet (for the founder and counsel to apply, not names):** clearable in restaurant, bar, brewery and beverage classes; no unfortunate meaning in Hindi, Urdu, Spanish or English; easy to say and spell for both communities; works beside the descriptor; does not trade on a real person or place; no direct overlap with an operating Austin food or beverage business; room for a beer line and a venue name; no alcohol-effect connotation.
