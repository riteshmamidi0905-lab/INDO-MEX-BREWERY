- NO facility is claimed available. No contact, booking or payment. No prices estimated; every figure is verbatim in the CSV with source and limits.

## What a Core 5 food test needs from a kitchen
Derived from `food/sessions/sessions.json` equipment_required, `docs/OPERATIONAL-REQUIREMENTS-CORE5.md` sections 1, 4, 6, and D-54. Planning figures are T4, unverified for Austin.
- [ ] Fryer with thermometer-controlled oil (Chicken 65 main load; Gobi optional); a vegetarian oil path or clean separation (cross-contact question).
- [ ] Comal / plancha / flat-top with measurable surface temperature (IR thermometer); quesadilla is the heaviest comal user.
- [ ] Charbroiler or very hot grill/broiler for paneer and chicken char; hood and fire rules are an open professional question.
- [ ] Oven for sealed-pot goat braise (hours) and cauliflower roast; 3 heavy lidded pots.
- [ ] Hot-hold at 135 F or higher (consomme, sauce).
- [ ] Cold storage including shallow-pan or ice-bath cooling for braised goat (planning: 135 to 70 F within 2 h, then to 41 F within 4 more h) and labelled storage.
- [ ] Separate raw-poultry workflow from vegetarian and ready-to-eat items; chicken to 165 F (Q-13, orientation only).
- [ ] Handwash sink and ware wash (3-compartment or machine); sanitiser test strips.
- [ ] Dry storage (masa, tortillas, spices) and allergen separation and labelling.
- [ ] D-54 control: no beef or pork products, stock, lard, or shared-fat history on oil and surfaces; ask what other tenants cook. No halal claim.
- [ ] Team brings: probe and IR thermometers, 1 g scale, timers.
- [ ] A certified food manager and handler training status for the test (Q-09; verify with the authority).

## Questions to ask each facility (when the founder decides; nothing contacted)
1. Is the listing current; which address and hours are real; is the kitchen operating now?
2. Is there a hood over the fryer and over the griddle/range; what type; can charred chile and frying run at once?
3. Exact equipment: fryer oil volume and thermostat control; griddle size and surface temperature range; oven type; hot-hold; ice machine; blast chiller?
4. Walk-in/reach-in space for goat cooling and storage: shelf space, shallow-pan capacity, temperature logs; is storage shared or lockable?
5. Is there a separate raw-poultry station or process; which allergens do other tenants handle; do others cook pork or beef or use lard, and can oil and surface separation be assured?
6. Which permit does the facility hold; does a tenant need its own Austin permit or work under yours; what documents are needed (insurance, food manager certificate, handler cards)?
7. Minimum hours, term, deposit; are one-off or trial sessions allowed; cancellation; availability at needed times; how is 24/7 access supervised?
8. Full price in writing including utilities, storage, cleaning, grease and waste, deposit, onboarding.
9. May outside guests attend a tasting; are events permitted; photography rules?
10. Insurance and liability requirements.
11. Ware wash, handwash sinks, dry storage allocation, loading and parking.
12. What happens to tenants if the host's inspection status changes?
