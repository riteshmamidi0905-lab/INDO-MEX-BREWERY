# Website as future validation infrastructure (ARCHITECTURE ONLY - nothing built, nothing deployed)
The site stays a static, `noindex` concept site (concept/prototype/tested/final labels). This note records how it could later help NASHA learn from real people, and what must exist first. **No sign-up form, analytics, cookie or personal-data capture is added now**, and no fake sign-ups are created.
| Capability | What it would learn | Data collected | Preconditions before building |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interest list | Whether anyone cares | email only, with a plain-language purpose | privacy notice reviewed by counsel; a real tool that stores data safely; a founder decision to go public (external action); name decision resolved (D-78) |
| Tasting signup / RSVP | Willingness to attend a real tasting | name, contact, party size, dietary and age flags | health/alcohol answers for the event (regulatory tree); real event date; consent wording |
| Dietary and beer/NA preference | What guests need | optional, non-identifying where possible | same; do not collect health data casually |
| Neighbourhood interest | Where to look | postcode only | data minimisation; no precise location |
| Waitlist or deposit test | Paid intent | payment data via a processor only | legal check on selling before licences; processor terms; refund policy |
Rules: collect the minimum; state the purpose; do not sell or share; honour deletion; keep the site free of any claim of reality (no photoreal concept image implying NASHA exists, real food or approved beer); keep `noindex` until the founder decides to publish; log any collection in the traction log (`validation/traction-event-log.csv`). The first real validation should not depend on the website at all (private tasting, invited people).
