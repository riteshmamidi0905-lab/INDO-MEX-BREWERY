- **Planning bands: BENCHMARK AGGREGATIONS, not estimates, not quotes** (sums of weak unit-cost ranges × concept areas; they **omit** kitchen equipment, boiler/CIP, glazing/canopy premium, court structure/cooling, play shade, sitework/utilities/stormwater, professional fees, permits/impact fees, FF&E, signage, pre-opening, **contingency, working capital, financing**):
| Scenario | Included lines only | Benchmark sum (low-high) | Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Core (hall, bar/kitchen block, compact outlet, land at Compact acreage) | land, restaurant block, production hall, 10-bbl-class brewing equipment, glycol, drains, outlet | ~$2.1M-$6.7M | **Benchmark** (T3 inputs) |

- **Why:** the Hefeweizen is the gate beer and nothing real can be learned without brewing; legality of serving pilot beer also matters.
- **Depends on:** F-2 (brewer and attorney). **Evidence now:** none beyond briefs. **Missing:** which brewers would partner, costs, TABC/TTB constraints on pilot/contract arrangements. **Cost:** TBD.
- **Options compared (all unvalidated; attorney/brewer to confirm):**
| Route | For | Against | Unknowns |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Partner brewery pilot** (brewer-designed batches made at a licensed brewery) | Licensed premises and equipment exist; expertise; real packaging/serving | Their equipment differs from ours; schedule/style fit; IP and terms; scale transfer | Terms, who holds permits for what, whether the beer can be sold at a pop-up (attorney) |
| **Dedicated small pilot system** | Control, repeatability, teaching tool | Needs licensed premises, capital, floor space; premature before a site | Permit path and cost (attorney, vendor) |
| **Homebrew-scale R&D** | Cheap idea screening | Not saleable or servable commercially; limited transfer | Only for internal sensory screening; legal limits to confirm |
| **Contract/collaboration brew with an established brewer** | Credibility, shared audience | Control and cost; collaboration depends on partner interest | Regulatory arrangement models exist; not researched |
- **Celis collaboration:** an **idea only**. There has been **no outreach and no indication of interest** from Celis. Do not imply otherwise.
- **Recommendation:** partner-brewery pilot, designed by the head brewer, starting with the Hefeweizen; defer a dedicated pilot system until a site exists. **Why:** least capital, fastest real evidence, keeps the Hefeweizen gate honest. **Next:** head brewer proposes candidate partners and a pilot plan; attorney confirms pop-up serving rules.
- **Owner:** Founder + head brewer. **Status:** WAITING on F-2. **Reversibility:** R1-R2.

## F-4 Cultural advisors
- **Why:** names, dish text, history cases and imagery must be reviewed before anything is public. **Depends on:** none (low cost, start early). **Evidence now:** criteria below; **no advisors named, none contacted.** **Cost:** TBD (honoraria/credit/fees to agree). **Reversibility:** R1.
- **Criteria for advisors:** lived or professional expertise in the relevant culture/region (e.g. South Indian/Telangana/Punjabi/Kerala culinary traditions; Jalisco/Oaxaca/Sonora/Mexico City; Indo-Mexican diaspora; food history); independence (no financial interest in the brand beyond fees); willingness to disagree in writing; mix of cooks, historians and community voices; no single advisor treated as representing a culture; diversity of region and generation.
- **Outreach structure (by role, not by names):** (1) 2 culinary advisors Indian-heritage, 2 Mexican-heritage; (2) 1 food historian; (3) 1 diaspora community voice. Sources to *look* (not claims of agreement): university South Asia/Latin America programmes, culinary schools, restaurateur associations, community cultural organisations, food-history scholars. Process: written brief → review packet (menu names and descriptions, claims audit, visual rules, brand rules) → structured feedback form → founder decision log recording where we followed or declined advice and why → right of dissent recorded.
- **Recommendation:** approve the criteria and start outreach by role before the pop-up. **Next:** founder approves criteria and roster size; names to be supplied by him or found through the sources above. **Owner:** Founder. **Status:** OPEN.
