# Questions for Professionals (attorneys and permitting authorities)

**Status:** `[PLAN]` question lists to hand to professionals. **No legal conclusions are given here and none has been obtained.** Facts quoted are tier T1/T3 only (`research/VERIFICATION-LEDGER.md`). Supports F-2 (authorising professionals) and the staged path (D-60). Separate specialists are likely: **Texas alcohol attorney**, **IP/trademark attorney**, and **permitting authorities** (DSHS/Austin Public Health, Austin Fire) who are not attorneys. Corporate/entity and tax counsel are also needed later.

## A. Texas alcohol attorney: beer without owning a brewery (early-stage questions)
Context we believe (unverified): TABC alcohol permits attach to a permanent physical address, so a mobile unit cannot hold one (V-26); third-party food trucks at a licensed premises count toward the holder's 60% sales test (V-27).
1. Can an unlicensed food business (truck, pop-up, brand) lawfully **partner with** a licensed brewery/taproom for residencies, pairing dinners and co-branded events? What arrangements are permissible vs prohibited (tied-house, financial interest, shared revenue)?
2. May a licensed brewery **reserve space or schedule** for our pop-up and market it jointly? Any limits on advertising or promotions that mention beer?
3. What can our **food-only** brand say or do about beer (pairing cards, "pairs with" suggestions naming a partner's beer) without violating TABC/TTB advertising rules?
4. **Collaboration beers:** how can a licensed brewery make a beer "with" us (recipe input, naming, co-branding)? Who must hold which permit/label (COLA), who owns the name/recipe, and what compensation is allowed?
5. **Contract brewing / alternating arrangements:** which structures exist in Texas and federally for a non-brewery brand to have beer made, and what do they require?
6. **Private tastings and events:** can we serve pilot or partner beer at a private tasting? When is a **temporary permit** needed and who holds it?
7. **Festivals and markets:** what do we need to be near alcohol sales at third-party events?
8. How should the **entity structure** separate a food business, a future brewpub and a possible beer brand (IP holding, liability)?
9. Does a future brewpub licence interact with an earlier food-only brand's history?
10. Texas **minors/arcade/play** points (carried from `business/AUSTIN-FEASIBILITY-V1.md` §13) once the flagship is in view.
11. Confirm the earlier T1 facts (production and self-distribution caps, off-premise containers) and what changed in 2025-26.
12. Confirm whether our use of "brewery" in a **name or marketing** before we hold a brewery licence is permitted and wise (`NASHA Brewing`, `NASHA Brewery & Kitchen` would be future uses).

## B. IP / trademark attorney
> *Sprint 6:* the name NASHA is founder-selected, clearance pending. Use the focused briefing and questions in `ip/NASHA-TRADEMARK-ATTORNEY-PACKAGE.md` first; the list below is the broader set.

13. **Name strategy:** run knockout and full searches (USPTO word/design, state, common law, domains, handles) for the parent name candidates (including founder-proposed **NASHA**) across beer, restaurant services, mobile food, retail and merchandise classes; rank risk. (USPTO states it will not run or interpret searches for applicants; common-law use can matter, V-29.)
14. **Filing strategy:** use-in-commerce vs intent-to-use; which marks (name, logo, tagline, beer names, "Labs") and when; budget; whether sub-brands (Kitchen, Brewing, Food Truck, Brewery & Kitchen, Labs) should be filed separately.
15. **Meaning and cultural/linguistic risk:** how should an attorney treat a name whose meaning in a source language may be sensitive (e.g. alcohol-related words)? Any registrability or consumer-perception issue?
16. **Copyright ownership:** for original website copy, photography, video, illustration, menu art, code: who owns what when created by us vs contractors? What written agreements are needed (assignment vs work-made-for-hire; contractor work generally needs a signed written instrument, V-28)?
17. **AI-assisted and AI-generated material:** how should we treat text drafted with AI assistance and images generated with AI tools (copyright eligibility, tool terms of use, disclosure, ownership claims)? What provenance records should we keep?
18. **Contractor and employee IP assignment** templates; moral rights; license-backs; confidentiality.
19. **Trade secrets:** what reasonable measures should we adopt for recipes and processes (access control, NDAs, marking), given Texas trade-secret law requires reasonable secrecy measures (V-30; current codified text unconfirmed)? Which assets are better as secrets than as published content?
20. **Patents:** is there any genuinely novel equipment/process that merits a patent search (we do not currently believe so)? How to avoid public disclosure that would prevent later filing?
21. **Website rights audit:** review the checklist in `ip/IP-OWNERSHIP-FRAMEWORK.md` §6; open-source licences, fonts, stock/generated imagery, music, analytics scripts.
22. **Founder control:** how to document that the **company** (not an individual or agency) owns domains, handles, repos, designs and lists; transferability for investors.
23. **Recipe and menu text:** what protection exists for original recipes and menu descriptions; what to publish publicly (build-in-public) vs keep private.

## C. Permitting authorities and regulators (not attorneys)
24. **DSHS / Austin Public Health:** current state Mobile Food Vending Permit process after the 2026 transition; commissary/CPF requirements and exceptions; certified manager rules; plan review for a trailer; farmers-market and event permits (V-23..V-25).
