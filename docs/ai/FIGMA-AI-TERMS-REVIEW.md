## Sprint 11 addition: provider (Google) terms, still T1
**Provider identity:** Figma's sub-processors page (https://www.figma.com/sub-processors/, retrieved 2026-10-11 via search summary) lists **Google LLC (Vertex)** and **OpenAI, LLC** under "AI Service Providers" for Figma AI, processing prompt inputs and outputs in the US; OpenAI also appears as a content-moderation sub-processor. The model we used is named `gemini-3.1-flash-image`; the exact serving route (Vertex or the Gemini API) is **not confirmed**.
**Figma's stance on providers (help center, possibly stale):** Figma does not permit third-party model providers to use customer content for training and limits vendor retention (https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/17725942479127).
**Google terms found (archived/secondary versions, not the current text):** Gemini API Additional Terms (archive, 2024): Google does not claim ownership of generated content but may generate the same or similar content for others; the user is responsible for use and for complying with law. Google Cloud Service Specific Terms: Google asserts no ownership in new IP in Generated Output; customers may not use Generated Output to develop a competing AI product; use is subject to the Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy; a **"Generative AI Preview Product" may be limited to evaluation and testing with no disclosure of output to third parties** unless Google permits otherwise in writing. Google retains prompts/outputs about 55 days for abuse monitoring.
**Open and decisive questions:** (a) Does Figma's customer contract (the AI Terms) govern output usage entirely, or do Google's incorporated restrictions bind the founder? Not established. (b) Is `gemini-3.1-flash-image` a "preview" model with evaluation-only limits? Unknown, and **that would matter for any public use**. (c) Prohibited-use policy compliance for the subjects shown (people, food, buildings): presumably fine, unreviewed. These keep every Figma-generated asset at **PROVISIONAL-LOCAL-ONLY**.
