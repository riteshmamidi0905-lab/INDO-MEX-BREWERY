## Rules specific to this strategy
1. Caveat in the post body, not only in a link. No number without its scope.
2. No link in post body (reach); put repo/case-study links in the first comment.
3. Never call a project production, deployed, or validated. Never imply the Apple engagement produced the open-source work.
4. Humanizer is style cleanup, not a claim of undetectability. Detection panel scores are local heuristics. State neither "undetectable" nor "human-written" about drafts. Rewrite structurally flagged lines yourself; the final voice must be yours.
5. Comments: write genuine ones, 2 options, you post them. No bulk/automated engagement, no scraping LinkedIn, no auto-connect, no DM sequences.
6. Recruiters/hiring managers: draft only; send nothing without your yes.
7. Measure after 30 days with `/li-audit` using your own LinkedIn analytics export. Until then no performance claim.
