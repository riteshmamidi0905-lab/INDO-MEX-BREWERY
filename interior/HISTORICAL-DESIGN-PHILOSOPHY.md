# Historical Design Philosophy

The interior should feel like **contemporary museum × world-class brewery × excellent restaurant × Austin hospitality**. History is the soul of the building, but the brewery remains the heart. `[CONCEPT]` All claims inside the displays follow `research/HISTORICAL-CLAIMS-AUDIT.md`.

## Principles
1. **Truth first.** Never invent history. Where there is no connection, say "no established connection". Where historians disagree, show the disagreement. Where we do not know, say so.
2. **Three story systems:** INDIA (independent), MEXICO (independent), CROSSINGS (defensible exchanges). Do not force them together.
3. **Objects, not sets.** Tell history with maps, ingredients, tools, photography, illustration, craft and food, in the same material language as the building (bone terrazzo, honed limestone, blackened steel, dark pecan, warm light). Never build palaces, temples, haciendas or ruins.
4. **Curate, do not overwhelm.** A small number of exceptional installations, rotated.
5. **Respect the difficult parts.** Colonialism (Spanish in Mexico, Portuguese and British in India), slavery and trade are acknowledged plainly, once, with dignity, in the Crossings Gallery. The brewery does not become a trauma museum or romanticize empire.
6. **No sacred decoration.** No deity statues, altars, photo-booth shrines, sacred iconography on beer, or religious objects as props. Religion appears only where historically essential, in context, with respect.
7. **Artifact ethics.** No sacred, archaeological, religious or culturally sensitive objects purchased as décor. Prefer commissions, licensed reproductions, museum-quality replicas, ordinary historic tools with documented provenance, photography, illustration and digital interpretation. Research provenance `[PRO]`.
8. **Beautiful if unread.** Every installation must work as a pure object/image even if no one reads a word.

## Four-level information hierarchy (every installation)
| Level | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A beautiful object or image | Works with zero reading |
| 2 | One sentence | ≤ 20 words, plain language |
| 3 | Short optional explanation | ≤ 60 words, on a small plaque or card |
| 4 | QR / digital archive | Sources, maps, longer text, source list, confidence marks |

## Typography and language
- One contemporary grotesque sans for captions and a restrained serif for headings; **no** fake ancient fonts, pseudo-Devanagari English type, stereotypical Mexican fonts, Western saloon fonts or novelty ethnic fonts.
- Spanish, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil or any other language appears only where contextually appropriate and **professionally verified**; never as decoration `[PRO]`.

## Do-not list
Flags; glowing world maps; sombreros, turbans, saris as props; Taj Mahal, Aztec calendar, elephants, mandalas, Día de los Muertos props; folk-art kitsch; "authentic" signage.
