# Interactive History (families)

> HISTORICAL (Track A concept): not evidence and not scoped to the Core 5 development program.

Physical first, screens last. All content follows the claims audit.
- **Ingredient drawers:** wooden drawers of grains to touch (barley, wheat, maize colours, rice, millets); a short tag per drawer.
- **Smell jars:** cumin, coriander, black pepper, chile, vanilla, cacao, lime.
- **Mechanical map:** a table map with sliding markers for crop origins; only verified routes.
- **Fermentation demonstrations:** a clear jar of a sourdough or ferment bubbling (simple brewing science), staffed by brewers on schedule.
- **Optional digital piece (not now): THE INGREDIENT JOURNEY.** Children and parents trace maize, chile, wheat, mango, cacao, spices across geography. **Do not build now.** Data must come from `research/INGREDIENT-JOURNEYS.md`; unresolved routes are not playable.
- **Where:** Game Hall (a small optional cabinet), Adventure Court edge (touch and smell), Outlet (a children's book corner). Not in dining rooms.
