# India Story System (independent)

> HISTORICAL (Track A concept): not evidence and not scoped to the Core 5 development program.

India is not reduced to the Taj Mahal, Mughal imagery, curry, elephants or mandalas. We curate across periods and regions and leave most things out. Status of each theme comes from the audit.

| Theme | Content idea | Evidence now | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Indus Valley kitchens** | Starch evidence for turmeric, ginger, wheat, barley, millets, eggplant, mango at Farmana c. 2600-2200 BCE; display as magnified starch-grain photographs and a reproduction pot | S-63 | VERIFIED, ready for design |
| **Grains and millets** | Specimens and botanical drawings of millets, wheat, barley, rice | S-63 for millets/wheat/barley | Partly ready |
| **Clay ovens** | Tandoor-type ovens have very old roots in South and West Asia; origin debated | S-73b (C) | UNRESOLVED; careful wording |
| **Spices and the pepper coast** | Black pepper and the Malabar trade | Not yet sourced | Research |
| **Chile arrives** | Chile reaches India in the 1500s | S-45 | SUPPORTED; shared with Crossings |
| **Regions** | Telangana/Hyderabad, Kerala, Goa, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Punjab, Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Northeast | Culinary benchmarks exist; history not researched | Rotating dining-room stories, one or two at a time |
| **Fermentation** | Northeast India rice beers (apong, zutho etc.) | S-74a | VERIFIED but needs community consultation |
| **Crafts** | Metalwork, ceramics (e.g., black pottery traditions), textiles | Not yet sourced | Research; commission contemporary craftspeople |
| **Colonial history** | Portuguese Goa (1510), British rule | Background | Acknowledge plainly in Crossings |
| **Modern India** | Cities, markets, migration, music | Not researched | Contemporary photography commissions |

Rules: no deity imagery; no flags; Telangana appears as a culinary and cultural *research region*, not a claimed heritage (D-09).
