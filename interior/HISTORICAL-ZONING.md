# Historical Zoning (story by zone)

> HISTORICAL (Track A concept): not evidence and not scoped to the Core 5 development program.

| Zone | Story | Installation | Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Entry: Two Worlds** | One sentence introduces two civilizations and one table | Dark threshold room, a single large photograph, no flags, no glowing map | Low |
| **History Corridor: Crossings** | Ingredients, trade, migration | Cases; map lines; Chile Wall alcove | Medium-high |
| **Tank Cloister: Fermentation** | From ancient grain to modern brewing | Grain & Fermentation Wall at the corridor end; view of the real tanks | High (the climax) |
| **Bar: Beer** | Beer ingredients; Indian, Mexican and American craft; wheat beer | Shadow boxes of barley, wheat, maize, hops; a few historic brewing tools | Medium |
| **Main dining: Regions** | One or two regions at a time (rotating) | A single limestone gallery band with prints and objects; rotate quarterly | Low-medium |
| **Kitchen: Fire** | Tandoor, comal, wood oven, charcoal | A restrained display of reproduction vessels near the pass; the real fire is the exhibit | Low |
| **Shade Court: Land and gathering** | Landscape, climate, agriculture, community | Planting palette and a small agricultural-history marker; no text clutter | Low |
| **Game Hall: Light history** | Visual, optional, fun | Optional "ingredient journey" cabinet; no reading walls | Low |
| **Adventure Court: Exploration** | Movement, geometry, nature | **No monuments or sacred architecture**; natural materials | None |
| **Flagship Outlet: Cultural design store** | Books, maps, prints, glassware, artists | Gallery-like shelving | Medium |
Rule: not every region everywhere; rotate; resist stuffing history into every room.
