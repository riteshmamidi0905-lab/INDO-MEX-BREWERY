# Mexico Story System (independent)

> HISTORICAL (Track A concept): not evidence and not scoped to the Core 5 development program.

Mexico is not reduced to Aztec imagery, tacos, sombreros, cactus or Día de Muertos. We curate across civilizations and regions.

| Theme | Content idea | Evidence now | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Maize** | Domesticated from teosinte in southwestern Mexico about 9,000 years ago; heirloom colours as specimens | S-64 | VERIFIED |
| **Nixtamalization** | Alkaline cooking of corn; UNESCO-recognized cuisine foundation | S-66, S-34 | VERIFIED (no date) |
| **Chile** | Domesticated in Mexico; central-east region most likely; dried chile varieties | S-59 | VERIFIED |
| **Traditional Mexican cuisine** | UNESCO 2010: maize, beans, chile, milpa, chinampas, grinding stones; collectives of female cooks | S-66 | VERIFIED |
| **Comal** | Nahuatl *comalli*; used since Mesoamerican times; earliest date disputed | S-73a | VERIFIED name/use; date UNRESOLVED |
| **Civilizations** | Olmec, Maya, Zapotec, Mixtec, Purépecha, Mexica | Not researched | Research; commission Mexican historians |
| **Regions** | Oaxaca, Yucatán, Jalisco, Puebla, Veracruz, Baja, Sonora, Michoacán, Mexico City | Culinary benchmarks exist; history not researched | Rotating dining-room stories |
| **Fermentation** | Tesgüino (maize beer; Tarahumara); pulque unresolved | S-74b | Consult communities; pulque research first |
| **Sonoran wheat** | Spanish wheat; flour tortilla tradition | S-48 | Supported |
| **Colonial history** | Spanish conquest and colony; Manila galleon | S-61 | Acknowledge plainly |
| **Modern Mexico** | Contemporary cuisine (Quintonil, Pujol), craft beer, migration | S-19, S-20, S-26 | Ready for contemporary features |
Rules: no sacred artifacts, no replicas of sacred codices as decoration, no Aztec calendar motif.
