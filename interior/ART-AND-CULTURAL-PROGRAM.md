# Art and Cultural Program

> HISTORICAL (Track A concept): not evidence and not scoped to the Core 5 development program.

- **Permanent commissions:** Indian, Indian-American, Mexican, Mexican-American and Austin artists. Themes: migration, grain, fire, fermentation, cities, family, food, land, water, music, work.
- **Rotating exhibitions** in the History Corridor and the Outlet gallery.
- **Do not** download or reuse cultural art; commission or license it with fair payment and credit.
- **Artifact ethics:** see `HISTORICAL-DESIGN-PHILOSOPHY.md` (no sacred or archaeological objects; provenance research `[PRO]`).
- **Advisory board:** two historians (South Asia, Mesoamerica), one food historian, one curator, community advisors; paid.
