# Investor Story Architecture v1

**Status:** `[STRUCTURE]`. This is the skeleton of a future investor narrative, **not a pitch deck and not an offering**. Nothing here may be shown to an investor as evidence unless its status is raised in `docs/readiness/VALIDATION-SCOREBOARD.md`. Each section states **what we can say today**, **evidence level**, and **what is missing**. Evidence levels: **E0** none · **E1** concept/design only · **E2** secondary research (T1-T3) · **E3** primary/external or tested · **E4** professionally reviewed/costed. Rule: a sophisticated hospitality investor must be able to tell real from assumed from unvalidated on every page. No securities, return or valuation language is used or implied; capital formation requires legal counsel.
