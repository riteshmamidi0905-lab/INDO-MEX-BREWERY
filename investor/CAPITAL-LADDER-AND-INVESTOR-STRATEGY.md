## 1. The staged company hypothesis and what must be researched to test it
| Stage | Name | What exists in it | Hypothesised benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Digital brand | Concept, brand, website, content, community, founder story | Attention and memory at low cost |
| B | Market validation | Interviews, private tastings, pop-ups, events, collaborations | Real demand signals before big spending |
| C | Mobile MVP | Trailer/truck or equivalent, limited menu, possibly brewery collaborations instead of owned equipment | Sales data, customers, margins |
| D | Proven hospitality brand | Repeat customers, events, partnerships, validated menu, merchandise, legal beer collaborations | Credibility |

**Must be researched before trusting the path:** (1) legal: how beer can be involved before a brewery (attorney); (2) operational: whether a mobile operation can show the right things about a flagship concept (a food truck may under-represent the pairing/brewery thesis); (3) financial: whether a small-format proof reduces or merely delays total capital needs; (4) brand: whether a mobile brand transfers to a destination brand; (5) opportunity cost and founder bandwidth; (6) whether investors actually value mobile traction for a brewpub (ask them later, not assume).
