## Phase 0: dummy-ring test (cheap, reversible, no logo)
Buy off-the-shelf **unbranded** weizen, conical lager and pilsner glasses. Fit a **removable** food-safe silicone band or heat-shrink at about 25% of the height as a stand-in ring (never glue, cut, etch or heat the glass). Test grip and wet hand feel, finger and lip distance, pour a test beer for foam, run a nesting/storage trial and a soil-and-rinse cleanability check. A removable band cannot test thermal shock, tempering or moulding: those stay a vendor and glass-engineer question. No logo or NASHA text (D-66).
**Warning on renders:** the site's glass drawings and the C6 image are not dimensioned drawings, show no wall thickness, seam or ring-as-bulge, and must not be quoted for scale, comfort or cleanability. `GLASSWARE-V2.md` mentions a "screen-printed or etched ring mark": that must be a plain ring line, not a logo or mark (D-66).
