Refines the ten Phase One concepts (kept in `phase-one/07-glassware-and-rituals.md`). One idea carries the family: **a raised glass ring (the "collar")** that echoes the tank's marigold band and the ring-in-square mark. It is the thing a guest photographs and the thing they feel under their fingers. Beer performance comes first.

## Why the base shape stays traditional (research)
- Tall, narrow-based weizen glasses with a wider top are described as helping build and hold a firm foam head and release the beer's fruity-spicy aromas; typical capacity 300-500 ml leaves room for head. `[V S-57 (dimensions.com), search-surfaced; retailer-grade]`
- Head retention relates to nucleation sites versus surface area at the top, per one beer blog `[V S-57, inference, C-grade]`. Therefore the collar must **not** add rough nucleation points inside the glass; it is an *outer* ring only. `[TEST: foam stability with and without collar]`

## Manufacturing reality (limited research)
- **Custom-mold routes (one supplier's published figures `[V S-56]`):** machine-molded: ~75,000-100,000 units minimum, $40,000-$60,000 mold, 6-8 months; mix-and-match (stock bowl/foot components): ~35,000 units, $15,000-$25,000, ~6 months; semi-automatic (made in China): ~10,000 units, $2,000-$5,000 mold, 4-5 months, capacity varies slightly per glass.
- **Decorated stock shapes:** promotional suppliers list 12-50 piece minimums, 5-7 days to 3 weeks, small setup fees `[V S-56 results]`.
- A single brewpub needs perhaps a few thousand glasses; custom molds at 10,000+ units imply **merchandise sell-through or a multi-site plan** `[C]`.
- **Staged path:** (1) Opening: a stock premium weizen glass with a **screen-printed or etched ring mark** (low MOQ) while the brand is tested; (2) after traction, commission the collar glass via a semi-automatic route (≥10,000 units) and sell it as merchandise; (3) at scale, move to machine-molded manufacturing.
- Rastal, Spiegelau, Stölzle, Libbey custom programs: not found in search `[Unresolved: contact directly]`.
- **Dishwasher / durability:** a thickened collar can create thermal stress; commercial dishwasher breakage testing and tempering options `[TEST/PRO: glass engineer]`.
- **Storage:** weizen vases are not nestable; plan racks and pantry height.
