# Beer Brand Experience v1 (consumer-facing)

**Status:** `[PROPOSED]`. The technical pilot briefs (`pilot-briefs/`) and program (`OPENING-BEER-PROGRAM-V1.md`) stay separate and authoritative on specifications. **No beer has been brewed or tasted; all descriptions say what a beer is *designed* to be.** The Hefeweizen is the gate beer: its public story must not be launched before it passes its gate. Glass family: `GLASSWARE-V2.md` (GLS-01/02 not yet generated).

## 1. Experience principles
1. **Approachable first:** a guest who knows nothing should be able to choose in 30 seconds. 2. **Classics first, regional beers earned.** 3. **Every beer has a job at the table** (pairing). 4. **Say what it is in plain words**, not tasting-note poetry. 5. **No novelty ingredients without a stated sensory job.** 6. **Honest:** pilot/seasonal beers are labelled as such.

## 2. Tap-board concept **[Proposed]**
Grouped by **how it drinks**, not by style jargon, with the plain style name always visible.
| Group | Beers (planned) | Reader cue |
|---|---|---|
| **Light & crisp** | Mexican-Style Lager, Pilsner | "Cold, clean, easy" |
| **Wheat** | Hefeweizen, Witbier, Dunkelweizen (seasonal) | "Soft, aromatic" |
| **Toasty** | Vienna Amber | "Bread-crust, balanced" |
| **Our house regionals** | Deccan Sol, Goa Baja (summer), Malabar Dark pilot (winter) | "Brewed with an ingredient that has a job" |
| **Non-alcoholic** | House aguas + a licensed NA beer | "No alcohol" |
| **Flex** | Reserve/guest (legality unresolved) | "Today only" |
Each line: name · plain style · ABV (**only after the beer exists**) · glass icon (collar) · pairing hint ("with Chicken 65 Totopos"). Heat/pairing symbols shared with the food menu. Digital tap board and a printed card; both show "pilot" where applicable.

## 3. Beer naming architecture **[Proposed]**
Public name = **plain style name** (+ optional short seasonal name later). Internal working names (SONA, HAWA, ANGARA) are not public until trademark-checked. Regional beers get a short descriptive name tied to the ingredient job; advisors review. **No** culture puns, deities, symbols or costume imagery. Series: **Classics · House Regionals · Reserve**.

## 4. Storytelling (templates; no flavour claims before tasting)
- **Hefeweizen:** "A traditional Bavarian-style wheat beer, designed to show banana and clove in balance. Poured in two steps." *[intent; gate pending]*
- **Mexican-Style Lager:** "A crisp, clean lager designed to be the cold partner for chile heat."
- **Vienna Amber:** "Toasted bread-crust malt, designed to meet char and braise."
- **Deccan Sol / Goa Baja / Malabar Dark:** one honest line on the ingredient and its job, e.g. "Brewed with toasted sesame and coriander seed for aroma." *[pilot; only if it passes foam/stability and TTB review]*
Beer stories avoid history claims beyond `research/HISTORICAL-CLAIMS-AUDIT.md` audited wording.

## 5. Tasting flights **[Proposed]**
Four 4-oz pours in the collar tulip on a pecan board with four cut-outs. Flights (from the program): **First Light** (Hefe, Hawa, Witbier, Pilsner), **The Crossing** (Hefe, Deccan Sol, Goa Baja, pilot regional), **Smoke & Amber** (Vienna, Dunkelweizen, Malabar Dark, Reserve), **Brewer's Table** (rotating five). A **pairing flight** option pairs three beers with three small plates. Pricing TBD (price survey incomplete).

## 6. Pairing system **[Proposed; untested]**
Three rules guests can remember: **Cold & bubbly for fat; low bitter beside heat; toast for char.** Each dish lists a primary and a backup beer; each beer card lists two dishes. Flavour reasons come from the program (carbonation vs fat, low bitterness vs capsaicin, malt toast vs char) and are **hypotheses until the pairing trials run**.

## 7. Serving ritual **[Proposed]**
**Two-Pour Hefeweizen:** pour ~80-85% at an angle, rest, swirl the yeast, finish upright; presented in the Collar Weizen. Rinse glasses cold. Flights arrive with a one-line card per beer. No fruit garnish by default.

## 8. Glassware strategy
Collar Weizen (0.5 L), Collar Lager (0.4 L), Stepwell Goblet (reserve), Collar Tulip (4 oz). The ring is a physical raised clear-glass collar, also used on tap handles and the tank sleeve. **Manufacturing, durability and cost are unvalidated**; staged path: stock premium glass with a printed ring mark first, custom mould only with sell-through (`GLASSWARE-V2.md`).

## 9. Beer education (non-intimidating) **[Proposed]**
A one-page "How to taste a wheat beer" card; a "Heat & Beer" pairing card; brewer's table nights; a brew calendar; on the website a **beer explorer** (see website IA) that lets a guest pick a mood or a dish and see matching beers. **No scoring or jargon walls.**

## 10. Plain descriptions for non-experts (templates)
"Light, crisp, refreshing." · "Soft, a little fruity and spicy-sweet (banana and clove)." · "Toasty, like bread crust." · "Dark, smooth, a little chocolatey." Written only after the beer is brewed and approved by the panel.

## 11. Unresolved
Gate beer result; guest-beer legality; packaged beer path; NA offering; hop-forward demand; TTB formula status for ingredient beers; naming trademark clearance; pricing.
