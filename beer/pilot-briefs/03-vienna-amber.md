## 1. Intended sensory identity
A copper-amber lager of toasted bread crust and light caramel with a clean, dry finish and **no roast**: the food-pairing workhorse for char, braise and tandoor (birria, lamb taco, dal-frijol). Toast, not sweetness. Mexico's Vienna-style lineage (for example Dos Equis Ámbar, described as Vienna-style, `S-49c`) is the reason for the style; the beer is not a copy.

## 3. Unacceptable faults (any detectable at panel = batch review)
- Roast or burnt notes; cloying caramel/sweetness
- Diacetyl, DMS, acetaldehyde, sulfur
- Thin body; muddy colour; dull finish
- Hop bitterness that fights chile heat (keep restrained)

## 6. Sensory panel structure
- **Panel:** at least 5 trained tasters (brewer, chef, two guests with beer training, one outsider), blind, randomised order, water and plain cracker between samples.
- **Samples:** pilot beer plus 2-3 commercial reference beers (below) plus one duplicate for consistency.
- **Scoring:** 0-5 per attribute (toasted crust, caramel, roast, sweetness, bitterness, clarity, body, finish), plus overall quality and "would order a second" yes/no.
- **Records:** `beer/pilot-log/` (create when brewing begins): batch ID, date, observations, scores, decisions.

## 7. Reference beers (choose and record at P2; availability and exact specs to be confirmed)
- Dos Equis Ámbar (cited as Vienna-style; confirm availability and current recipe)
- BJCP commercial examples to be pulled from the 7A style page; a Texas Vienna lager if one exists (select and record at P2)

## 8. Pilot questions to answer
1. Which malt strategy gives toast without sweetness (head brewer's design)?
2. Does it match the goat birria and lamb taco better than the Dunkelweizen or Hefeweizen in blind pairing?
3. How long must it lager in our equipment?
4. Does Austin water alkalinity suit amber malts (usually more forgiving than pale lagers: verify)?
5. Does it stay drinkable at 4-6 °C with chile-forward dishes?

## 9. Pass / fail gate
**PASS:** faults 0; toast/crust scored 2-3 with roast 0; panel average ≥ reference; wins at least 2 of 3 pairing trials with birria, lamb taco and dal-frijol dip; consistent across two batches. **FAIL:** revise malt/water/lager plan; do not add colour to fake maltiness.

## 10. What remains the head brewer's professional responsibility
Recipe design (grist, hops, adjuncts), mash and water strategy, yeast selection and pitching, fermentation and conditioning schedules, lager time, carbonation and packaging, sanitation and safety, QC methods and limits, scale-up, TTB/regulatory formula questions, and the decision to proceed or stop. This brief gives the intent and the gates only.
