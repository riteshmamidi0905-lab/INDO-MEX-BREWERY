# Brewer Handoff Package (Sprint 3)

**For:** a licensed/qualified head brewer or brewing consultant `[PRO]`. **Status:** nothing has been brewed; no pilot results exist; no recipe is supplied. This page orients a brewer and lists the questions we need answered. Details: `01-hefeweizen.md`, `02-mexican-style-lager.md`, `03-vienna-amber.md`; full program `beer/OPENING-BEER-PROGRAM-V1.md`.

## 1. The concept in brewer terms
A destination brewpub in Austin, Texas: visible brewhouse, six fermenters (visual canon; real size undecided), a kitchen cooking Indian- and Mexican-inspired food (**no beef, no pork**; goat, lamb, chicken, fish, many vegetarian dishes), a shaded outdoor court, family facilities phased later. Beer philosophy: **classics first; experiments later; every unusual ingredient needs a sensory job; no novelty for its own sake.** The Hefeweizen is the proof beer: if it fails, nothing else launches. Beers are meant to pair with chile-forward, fatty, smoky food.

## 2. Priority beers and roles
| # | Beer | Role | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hefeweizen (BJCP 10A) | Flagship, credibility test | Benchmark panel + 3 consistent batches |
| 2 | Mexican-Style Lager (BJCP 2A as nearest) | Session beer for chile food | Clean, crisp, faults 0; beats Hefe as partner for ≥2 heat-2 dishes |

Later (not yet briefed): Pilsner, Witbier, Dunkelweizen/Malabar Dark, Deccan Sol, Goa Baja. Ingredient-driven beers need foam, stability and **TTB formula** review first (tier T1: exempt list not yet checked for kokum, tamarind, hibiscus, mango, saffron, chile; `research/VERIFICATION-LEDGER.md` V-09).

## 3. Service context
Draft first (kegs); glass family: 0.5 L weizen glass, 0.4 L lager glass, 4 oz flight glass; Two-Pour ritual for the Hefeweizen; serving temperatures are targets only. Packaging (cans) is a possible later phase, not Phase A.

## 4. Known constraints
- Austin water: soft and mildly alkaline per search summaries (hardness ~70-126 ppm, alkalinity ~50-80 mg/L, pH ~9.6; **T3, lab test needed**); pale lagers will need your water strategy.
- Texas licensing is unresolved (attorney): pilot legality, guest beer, packaging, distribution.
- Wastewater: Austin Water General Industrial User permit and pH/temperature limits apply (T1, V-08).
- Visual canon tanks are ~50 bbl working each; demand (BA median 2.6 bbl/seat/yr, T3) suggests ~780 bbl/yr at 300 seats; **vessel size is yours to recommend** (D-51).
- Dietary policy: **no beef/pork derivatives**; **no gelatin fining**; fish-derived finings (isinglass) are an open vegetarian-labelling question.
- Founder priorities: Hefeweizen quality; honest scale; pairing with the menu; nothing launched before its gate.

## 5. What is explicitly yours (professional responsibility)
Recipe design (grist, hops, adjuncts), mash/water, yeast selection and pitching, fermentation and conditioning schedules, lager time, carbonation, packaging, sanitation, QC methods/limits, scale-up, equipment compatibility, regulatory formula questions, and the decision to proceed or stop. We will not impose numbers.

## 6. Questions we need you to answer
1. **Fermenter size and count** for a ~330-seat destination: what do you recommend (we model 15- and 30-bbl scenarios), brew-day cadence, and brewhouse size relation? Does six fermenters make sense, plus brite/serving tanks?
2. **Pilot route:** partner brewery, small pilot system, or other, given legality and equipment differences? What scale transfers to a ~15-30 bbl system for a Hefeweizen?
3. **Hefeweizen yeast and fermentation strategy** you would trial (we have only datasheet candidates), and which sensory/analytical measurements you want.
4. **Lager time and tank-time** you realistically need for Mexican-Style Lager and Vienna; does our tank-time budget (`OPENING-BEER-PROGRAM-V1.md` §6) look credible?
5. **Water:** what testing and treatment plan; what water do you need to know first?
6. **Packaging path:** keg-only first; what is the minimum equipment to add cans later; CO₂ and glycol implications?
7. **Pilot batch plan:** how many batches, volumes, timeline, cost, and what you would need from us.
8. **Quality system:** QC lab minimum, sensory panel setup, record-keeping, shelf-life testing.
9. **Ingredient beers:** which of Deccan Sol / Goa Baja / Malabar Dark are feasible, what the foam/stability risks are, and the TTB formula route.
10. **Staffing:** what brewing team and training does a visible brewery need; safety and guest-facing duties.
11. **Utilities and room needs** for the brewhouse/cellar (CO₂, glycol, steam, drains, trade-waste pretreatment) to pass to the architect.
12. What did we not ask that we should have?

## 7. What we will give you
This package, the three pilot briefs, the beer program, the menu test set, the Locked Scene concept images (illustrative only), and the dietary policy. We would like back: a scoped proposal with costs (we hold none), recommended vessel sizing with reasoning, and a pilot plan.

## 8. Dependencies
Vessel-size answer → architect brief and capex model; pilot-route answer → pilot timeline and cost; attorney answer → what pilot batches may legally be served at a pop-up.
