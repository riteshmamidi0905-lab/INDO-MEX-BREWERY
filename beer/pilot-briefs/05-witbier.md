# Pilot Brief: Witbier (working name not yet chosen)

**Status:** brewer-facing brief, written Sprint 16. **Nothing has been brewed.** Specification of intent and test plan, NOT a recipe: no grist, spice doses, peel weights or yeast choices are fixed. A qualified brewer owns formulation. Style numbers are BJCP 24A style-typical (T1) or `[TEST]`/T4.

## 1. Intended sensory identity
Hazy pale, soft and light-bodied, with the style-traditional orange-peel and coriander as aroma only, supported by a Belgian wit yeast. Role: seasonal classic (Mar-Sep) and the citrus-aromatic beer for paneer, vegetables and char. **Do not add spices or ingredients merely to look Indian**; coriander and orange peel are already style-traditional and their only job is aroma.

## 2. Style reference and ranges
| Item | Reference | R&D window `[TEST]` |
|---|---|---|
| SRM | 2-4 | 2-4 |
| IBU | 8-20 | 10-14 |
| Carbonation (T4) | about 2.5-3.0 vol CO2 | brewer to set |
| Body | soft, light; wheat and often oats | brewer to set |

## 3. Faults to exclude
Harsh or soapy coriander, perfume-like peel, excessive phenolics, lactic sourness beyond style, unstable haze (haze should look intentional), diacetyl.

## 4. Brewer questions
Spice dose and timing; peel sourcing and freshness; haze stability and sediment; yeast cross-contamination risk with the lager workflow (an ale yeast in a lager brewery); fit to the seasonal calendar; a Celis collaboration remains an open, unverified question.

## 5. Allergens, labelling, ingredients
Wheat/gluten (not gluten-free); coriander is an allergen-labelling item to confirm; TTB formula status of spices/peel is an attorney/regulatory check. No health claims.

## 6. Service and pairing hypotheses (NOT SENSORY TESTED)
