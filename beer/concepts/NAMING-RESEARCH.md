# Internal naming research: PAAPI, BADSHAH, AMBA HAZE (2026-10-22)

NOT a clearance search; not legal advice. Public-web summaries only (T1 at best). An attorney must run USPTO, Texas and common-law searches (D-62, D-79). No cultural advisor has reviewed any of this. All three stay INTERNAL working names; none is for public use or a finalized product.

## PAAPI (internal naming candidate)
- Verified with sources: Hindi "paapi" = "sinner" (from paap, "sin"; HinKhoj, WisdomLib, Rekhta); "paap" is a serious category in Hindu and Sikh usage (SikhiWiki "Paap"); Spanish "papi" = "daddy", affectionate or flirtatious (Dictionary.com). A Mumbai bar called Papi plays on both senses (Restaurant India).
- Judgement, UNCITED, needs advisors: in casual Hindi/Urdu/Punjabi "paapi" can be teasing or a real moral accusation; alcohol is already read as sinful in some Hindu, Muslim and Sikh circles, so a beer called "sinner" can read as mockery or as glamorising transgression (against the repo rule that excess is never glamorised); Spanish readers will hear "papi" (sexualised/cheesy); sin-and-virtue imagery must not become label decoration. It also duplicates the BADSHAH/SONA hefeweizen slot.
- Crowding signals (unverified snapshots): a coffee absinthe called "Paapi" (Goa, Curly Tales), a restaurant "Paapi Pet" (Rourkela), US filings for PAPI including beer and soft-drink goods (Justia 88846124, 98394867, 88985328); PAPI and PAAPI are phonetically identical.
- Screening opinion: HIGH risk. Prefer a neutral alternative; send to advisors (Hindi/Urdu/Punjabi speakers, a Sikh and a Muslim reviewer, a Mexican-American reader) before any more design investment.

## BADSHAH (existing hefeweizen identity, preserved)
Living musician's stage name, who launched a vodka brand (Shelter6, 2025; Storyboard18); Badshah Masala is a food company founded 1958 (YourStory); common word in South Asia; D-18 steers away from royalty names. No Badshah-branded beer found in one search (absence is not availability). Screening opinion: HIGH trademark/persona risk; keep internal; attorney to judge right-of-publicity.

## AMBA HAZE (working name for the experimental mango hazy IPA)
"Amba" is a mango word in several Indian languages. A South-Asian-inspired sour ale called "Amba" exists (Peabody Heights Brewery, Baltimore); DESTIHL sells an "Amra" mango IPA (different word, same theme). No "Amba Haze" beer found in a thin search; "haze" is descriptive. Screening opinion: MEDIUM; fine as a handle, not as a brand.
