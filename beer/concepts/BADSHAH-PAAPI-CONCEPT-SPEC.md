## AMBA HAZE (working name): experimental mango-forward hazy IPA (hypothesis)
Style: nearest BJCP 2021 21B7 Hazy IPA (UNCITED). OG ~1.060-1.075, FG ~1.010-1.016, ABV ~6.0-7.5%, IBU ~25-50 (low perceived), SRM ~3-6, CO2 ~2.2-2.6 vol. Grain: pale/2-row base, 20-30% flaked oats/wheat (gluten-containing; no gluten-free claim). Hops: late/dry-hop weighted tropical and stone-fruit varieties chosen by the brewer. Yeast: fruity low-flocculation ale strain. Mango: aroma-hop-only has no refermentation risk but no real mango; puree/fruit adds fermentable sugar and microbial load (refermentation, over-carbonation, burst packages, pH drift); extract/natural flavor needs TTB/label review. Hypothesis: hop-driven base with a small blind puree trial. Process risks [PRO]: oxidation, hop creep, haze stability, pectin/line issues, grassy off-notes, cold chain, packaging. Sensory target: ripe mango/peach/tropical, opaque gold-orange, pillowy, short juicy finish, no heat (habanero stays out of the beer). Serve 4-7 C. Glass: collar tulip or stepwell goblet (`beer/GLASSWARE-V2.md`); full-size IPA glass undecided. Benchmarks to study: Tree House Julius, Trillium Congress Street, Other Half Green City, Monkish, Sierra Nevada Hazy Little Thing. Pairing hypotheses (AMBA HAZE): Chicken Tikka Nachos (fruit vs char), Chicken 65 Totopos, Mango Habanero Gobi (harmony vs sweet-on-sweet and capsaicin amplification: test with/without glaze), Nawaabi Enchiladas (likely clash with cream: test).
