# NASHA — BRAND SYSTEM (VISION LAYER)

*VISION. D-66 stands: no final logo, no ™ or ®. The word NASHA is a temporary typographic placeholder. The name is not legally cleared, so every concept below must survive a rename.*

## Uniforms (conceptual)
Austin hospitality: functional, warm, modern, with a quiet identity.
- **Floor and bar:** charcoal canvas apron over a bone shirt; a canvas pocket (no leather); comfortable shoes.
- **Brewery:** black work shirt, work trousers, rubber boots, safety eyewear and gloves where the task needs it.
- **Kitchen:** dark cotton chef jacket, plain cap, heat-safe apron.
- No costumes, no regional dress, no slogans.

## Signage system (conceptual, no spend)
Three positions, all material first: a blackened-steel panel on a concrete plinth by the entrance, a slim blade sign under the arcade, a door plaque. Text is a temporary placeholder. Nothing here implies trademark clearance. If the name changes only the lettering changes.

## Pattern grammar
One restrained system built from three simple structural ideas (designers can find parallels in many cultures; none is borrowed from a specific one): **perforation** (circles on a regular offset grid, like a stone or metal screen), **weave** (over-under stepping, like a mat or a textile) and **tile** (a square grid with a broken stripe). It should work on a menu, tile, a web background, a coaster, fabric, a screen and packaging. It is not copied from any sacred, ceremonial or protected motif. Cultural reviewers from both traditions must check it before real use.

## Packaging (concept drawings)
Takeout box with a woven paper sleeve; a paper bag with an indigo stripe; a coaster with the perforation pattern; a napkin in indigo and bone. Beer packaging is not drawn as a compliant label. No regulatory claim.

## Sound
Day quiet and warm; evening building. Never a stereotyped playlist. Conversation always possible. Austin's live-music identity is a question: a small alcove stage may host one acoustic set, with licensing and permits unresolved.

## Day to night
Day: limestone, light, greenery. Golden hour: the court warms. Night: brass, amber, the tanks glowing. No nightclub lighting at any hour.

## People
Diverse Austin adults, families, staff and brewers; no identifiable people; no minors with alcohol; children's zero-proof moments clearly separate.
