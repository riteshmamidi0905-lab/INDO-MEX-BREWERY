# NASHA — PRODUCT AND TABLE (VISION LAYER)

*VISION. Conceptual presentation only. None of these dishes, beers or drinks has been made. Asset classes: VISION FOOD — NOT COOKED PRODUCT; VISION BEER — NOT BREWED PRODUCT; VISION NA — NOT TESTED PRODUCT. No prices.*

## Food language (one system)
A real table. Warm directional light from one side. Texture. Steam only where honest. Hands now and then. A glass nearby. Controlled imperfection. Hospitality context. No isolated plate floating in black, no excess garnish, no impossible ingredients, no sterile studio, no tweezers. Plating is stoneware, a steel tray with paper, a small katori.

## Zero-proof (from the Sprint 24 flavour architecture)
Clear glass from the same family, natural colour, one simple garnish, equal care beside the beer.
- Chicken 65: fruit, lime and salt cooler (cold, bright, clear red-gold).
- Paneer taco: spiced-citrus herbal sparkler (pale green-gold, fine bubbles).
- Goat birria: hibiscus and tamarind spiced tea (deep ruby, cool or slightly warm).
- Gobi taco: lime-and-herb saline cooler (clear, pale, a green leaf).
- Butter chicken quesadilla: cultured dairy, fruit and spice drink (pale cream-apricot).
All are categories with a flavour plan, not recipes; none is tested. Avoid children's-soda colours, mocktail clichés, garnish towers.

## Glassware (vision)
Three core shapes tell the story more clearly than five. **Signature Hefe glass** (tall, a raised collar ring a quarter up), **Lager and Pils glass** (a refined cone), **Aromatic glass** (a stemmed short tulip for amber and witbier), plus a small flight tulip. Dishwasher and rack safety, vendor and manufacturability are **UNVERIFIED**. The existing five-profile concepts remain in the Blueprint (V-12).

## Serving ritual
Restrained. A clean glass on a coaster. For the Hefe, a two-step pour at the table only if testing proves it helps. No smoke, no dry ice, no flame. Materials only where sensible: brass touchpoints, ceramic and wood coasters, a woven fibre mat under a bread basket.

## The table
Plate (matte black stoneware), shared steel tray with paper, small katori for sauces, a woven basket for tortillas or totopos, indigo-and-bone napkin, flatware kept simple, two glasses (beer and zero-proof) plus a water glass, one cruet for condiments. Do not crowd it. The table should photograph without styling.

## Menu
One folded card. Order: **Food** first (five dishes), **Beer** beside, **Pairings** as an optional line under each dish ("Try this with…"), **Zero-proof**, **Discover** (flights, a taste before you commit). No prices, no allergen claims on the concept card. Pairing is always optional.

## Pairing language — plain words
| Technical idea | Say it like this |
|---|---|
| Palate cleansing | "Bright bubbles reset your mouth after each bite." |
| Contrast | "Cool and crisp against hot and rich." |
| Aroma bridge | "The beer smells like something already on the plate." |
| Acidity | "A squeeze of sourness keeps rich food light." |
| Bitterness | "A little bitter edge says ready for the next bite." |
| Heat relief | "It calms the chile without wiping it out." |
| Texture | "Soft and creamy against crisp and charred." |
Every pairing is a proposal until it has been tasted.
