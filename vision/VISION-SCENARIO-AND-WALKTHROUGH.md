# VISION SCENARIO AND INVESTOR WALKTHROUGH

## VISION SCENARIO — one future evening
*VISION SCENARIO. It communicates experience. It invents no sales, covers, staffing, revenue or customer behaviour. Nothing here has happened or is predicted.*

**5:30 PM — Doors open.** Low sun rakes across the court. The arcade shade is cool. A cook lights the oven; the first smoke drifts along the north arcade. A brewer checks a tank through the glass. At the bar the first glasses come off the shelf.

**6:00 — The early table.** Families come in. A child has a cup of juice while the parents share a tray of Chicken 65 Totopos. The room is bright, easy, and loud in the way a family room is.

**7:00 — The court fills.** The long table fills with friends. The rill runs. Light turns gold on the limestone. Someone says "what is that?" at the passing goat birria; the server says the consomme comes in a small cup, and describes the beer in four words.

**8:00 — The pairing hour.** Hefeweizen with the birria. A lager beside the totopos. A zero-proof tamarind tea at the next table. A couple compares a bite with a sip and laughs. The tanks begin to glow.

**9:00 — Social energy.** The arcade is warm brass. A solo guest at the bar watches the brew deck; the bartender explains. Plates move. The room is busy but you can still talk.

**10:00 — Late dinner and the bar.** The court quiets to small groups. A final plate of quesadilla wedges. The oven mouth glows. A brewer wipes down a valve. Staff stack the pecan chairs.
