## 3. India framework: which regions earn a place?

Selection test: does the region offer (a) a distinct ingredient/technique, (b) a natural bridge to beer (bitter, roasted, sour, ferment, smoke, malt), and (c) a design/story hook? India is not forced; regions that fail are parked, not erased.

| Region | Signature ingredients / techniques | Beer bridge | Design/story hook | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Telangana / Hyderabad | Chile, slow-cooked meat, dum/biryani, tamarind, sesame, peanut, curry leaf, Deccan plateau, Nizami heritage | Roasted malt with nut/sesame; amber lager with tamarind-sour pairing | Deccan stone, Charminar-era geometry (as principle only) | **CORE** (also the founder's roots; claim only what is true to the founder's story) |
| Andhra | Intense chile heat, gongura (sorrel), pickles | Wheat beer to temper heat; sour beer experiments | Pickle/jar culture | CORE (food > beer) |
| Kerala | Black pepper, coconut, curry leaf, seafood, banana, kokum-like kudampuli | Pepper is a real brewing adjunct; coconut; saison-like dryness | Backwaters, wood, brass lamps | **CORE** |
| Goa | Coastal, Portuguese influence (vinegar, pork), kokum, cashew, toddy-fermentation heritage | Coastal lagers, sour/vinegar notes | Colonial tile, azulejo-like tile culture (overlaps Mexican tile: a genuine bridge) | **CORE** |
| Punjab | Tandoor, butter, smoke, grilled meat, paneer, mustard greens | Smoke + malt; Vienna-style amber lager | Tandoor as hearth | **CORE** |
| Rajasthan | Dry-desert ingredients (lal maas chile, gram flour, dried legumes), forts/stepwells/jaali | Desert-dry crispness; stepwell geometry | Architecture reference | CORE (architecture > food) |

## 5. Pairing matrix

Rating: **A** = strong, build around it · **B** = good, selective use · **C** = forced, avoid.

| Pairing | Why it works | Shared / complementary | Technique bridge | Differences to respect | Beer | Food | Design | Forced-fusion risk | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Telangana × Jalisco** | Both: chile-driven, goat/mutton traditions, slow-cooked braise, dry-heat plateau/dry land agaves | Dried chiles, cumin, coriander seed, tamarind ↔ guajillo, sesame/peanut | Dum/braise ↔ birria consomé | Hyderabadi biryani layers rice; birria is soup + taco | Amber lager, malty Vienna-style | Goat birria with Hyderabadi-style masala technique | Deccan stone + agave landscape | "Garam masala dumped on birria." Mitigate: build the adobo from Indian dried chiles *as structure*, keep the consomé | **A** |
| **Kerala × Yucatán** | Both humid, coastal, tropical; coconut/citrus/banana leaf; pit/leaf-wrapped cooking | Banana leaf, coconut, sour fruit (kokum-like / sour orange), black pepper ↔ habanero | Steaming and roasting in leaf (pollichathu ↔ pibil) | Yucatán uses achiote/sour orange; Kerala uses pepper/coconut | Citrus/spice wheat, saison-like | Banana-leaf-wrapped fish, pibil-style pork | Wood, lamps, blue-ochre palette | Medium | **A** |
| **Goa × Baja California** | Coastal, fish, vinegar/acid, Portuguese/Spanish tile and fermentation | Vinegar, lime, coconut, pork | Recheado (spiced paste) ↔ adobo-style marinade | Baja is lighter/grilled; Goa heavier | Crisp coastal lager | Fish tacos with recheado | Tile (azulejo / Talavera) | Low | **A** |
| **Punjab × Sonora** | Wheat country, flour breads, grilled meat, dairy | Tandoor naan ↔ flour tortilla (tortilla de harina), carne asada ↔ tandoori | Live-fire | Tandoor is clay oven; Sonoran uses mesquite grill | Vienna lager, IPA | Tandoori carne, naan-tortilla hybrids | Fire as center | Low | **A** |
| **Rajasthan × Oaxaca** | Desert + highland, bold dried spice, craft textile traditions, stone architecture | Chiles, smoked dried ingredients, pinks/ochres | Dry roasting | Food less alike; design alike | Dry, crisp lagers | Selective | **Architecture lead**: stone, jaali ↔ lattice, color | Medium on food | **A (design) / B (food)** |
| **Tamil Nadu × Veracruz** | Coastal, pepper/tamarind, ferment, African/Spanish/Dravidian trade histories | Tamarind, pepper, seafood, tropical fruit | Fermented batters ↔ masa | Veracruz uses olives/capers (Spanish) | Pilsner, spiced lager | Veracruz-style fish with Chettinad pepper | Mansion courtyard | Medium | **B** |
| **Maharashtra × Mexico City** | Street-food capitals, pav ↔ bolillo/torta | Bread-and-filling culture | Street assembly | Mumbai vada pav vs CDMX tortas | Session lagers | Torta/pav hybrids | Street signage | Medium (gimmick risk) | **B** |
| **Gujarat × Puebla** | Sweet-sour-spicy balance; vegetarian + mole sweet-spicy-nutty complexity | Nuts, seeds, chocolate/jaggery, sweet-sour | Layered sauces | Gujarat veg vs Puebla pork | Dark lager, dunkel | Vegetarian mole-style dishes | Pattern (bandhani ↔ Talavera) | Medium | **B** |
| **Karnataka × Chiapas** | Coffee-growing regions | Coffee | Roasting | Chiapas food less documented in the research so far | Coffee stout | Coffee dessert | | Weak | **B-** (coffee only) |
| **West Bengal × Veracruz** | Fish, river/coast, sweets | Fish | | Mustard oil is hard to translate; flavors diverge | | | | High | **C** reject as a structured pairing; allow isolated dishes |

Decision: build the identity on the **five A-pairings** (Telangana×Jalisco, Kerala×Yucatán, Goa×Baja, Punjab×Sonora, Rajasthan×Oaxaca) and the architectural pairing Tamil Nadu (Chettinad) courtyards ↔ Mexican hacienda courtyards.
