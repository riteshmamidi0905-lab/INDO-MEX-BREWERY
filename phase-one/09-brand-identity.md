# 19. Brand Identity Directions

> HISTORICAL: ANGAN and other candidate names are not the brand. See brand/NASHA-BRAND-STRATEGY.md (D-62).

## Evaluation of "INDO MEX BREWERY" as a name
- **Pros:** instantly says what it is; strong search clarity.
- **Cons:** descriptive and probably hard to protect `[PRO]`; "Indo" can be read as Indonesia; "Mex" shortcut can feel casual or Tex-Mex; hard to premiumize; can't grow into a global brand with other categories.
- **Recommendation:** keep "Indo-Mex" as the *descriptor* (e.g., "ANGAN — Indo-Mex Brewery"), not the brand. Pick a parent brand after trademark searching.

## Identity system (CONCEPT)
- **Signature 1: The Glass.** Faceted weizen glass silhouette.
- **Signature 2: The Court.** Mark based on a square-in-square courtyard plan with a centered circle (tank from above): works as stamp, tap-handle top, can icon.
- **Signature 3: Palette.** Terracotta, brass, deep ink-blue, limestone cream, chili red as a rare accent, agave green as a secondary. Avoid saffron/green/white flags and avoid red-green-white tricolor (national flag references).
- **Signature 4: Pattern system.** Geometric lattice patterns *constructed* from rules (not copied from specific temples or monuments) so it is original and trademarkable.
- **Type:** a sturdy serif for names, a plain grotesque for information, hand-lettered beer names only if licensed from a designer.
- **Voice:** warm, precise, lightly witty, never mock-ethnic. No fake accents.
- **Packaging:** cans share a colored band and pattern; each region has its own pattern variation; Classics are simpler. Everything must work at shelf distance, in black-and-white, on a tap handle, and embossed on glass.
- **What to avoid:** deity imagery, caricatures, sombreros, turbans-as-costume, pseudo-Devanagari, and any real brand's colors.
