## Principles (fuse principles, not decorations)
| Source | Principle borrowed |
|---|---|
| Indian | Courtyard as the center of the house; jaali screens filter light and air; thick stone for thermal mass; brass and carved wood that age; water for cooling; shaded verandas |
| Mexican | Patio at center; plaster walls in strong color; terracotta and tile floors; volcanic stone; indoor/outdoor flow; framed sky |
| Austin/Texas | Industrial shed volumes, exposed steel, limestone, live oak shade, patios, stage |

## Three directions
**Concept 1: The Imperial Brewhouse.** Monumental entry, dark metal, warm brass, stone. Tanks as a cathedral nave. Pros: drama, premium. Cons: risk of palace-theme cliché, cost, cold feel.

**Concept 2: The Monsoon Courtyard.** Open-air courtyard, water, plants, terracotta, communal tables, night lighting. Pros: warm, communal, Texas-friendly. Cons: brew visibility weaker, weather exposure, tanks less heroic.

**Concept 3: The Deccan × Desierto Brewery.** Contemporary volumes, geometric screens, Mexican materiality (plaster, stone), Texas steel; huge glass brewing hall. Pros: most distinctive. Cons: could become cold/corporate if materials aren't warm.

## Recommended combined direction (Concept 4): **THE COURT BREWHOUSE**
Core move: a **glass-walled brewhouse at the center of a courtyard plan**. Visitors approach through a shaded, screened gateway and *the first thing they see is tanks*.

- **Plan:** Rectangular steel-framed hall (Texas shed), wrapped by two zones: (a) a 2-story interior hall with brewhouse in a glazed hall visible from the entrance, bar and kitchen around it; (b) an outdoor court (beer garden) on the other side, with water channel, live oaks/native planting, stage, communal tables.
- **Heroes:** polished stainless fermenters with architectural uplighting; copper-clad brewhouse kettles; brass fittings; visible piping painted/anodized in a single palette.
- **Skins:** Texas limestone base, terracotta-colored lime-plaster walls, laser-cut weathering-steel jaali screens (modern patterns derived from regional geometries rather than copied), tile only in discrete accents (bar front, restroom walls).
- **Light:** Dim warm interior; tanks as the brightest objects at night; dappled light from jaali screens by day.
- **Climate:** Shaded courtyards, cross-ventilation, ceiling fans, misters, large overhangs `[PRO]`.
- **Brew deck / catwalk:** Raised viewing deck with safe guarding. Feasibility needs structural and code review.
- **Barrel room:** Separate cool, quiet room with glass partition.
- **Continuity bible:** see `visuals/prompts/00-STYLE-BIBLE.md`.
