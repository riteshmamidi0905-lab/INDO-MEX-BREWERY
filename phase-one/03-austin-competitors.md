## Positioning map (CONCEPT hypothesis, to test)
Axes: **X = Beer rigor** (generic → obsessive), **Y = Cultural/culinary specificity** (generic pub food → distinct cuisine).
- High beer rigor, low culinary specificity: classic craft brewers (pizza/pub food, food trucks).
- Moderate beer rigor, medium culinary specificity: Lazarus-type (taco + beer).
- Low rigor, high specificity: ethnic restaurants with beer lists.
- **Unmet quadrant (hypothesis):** high beer rigor × high culinary specificity × a large, designed space. A brewery where the kitchen is as serious as the brewhouse, and the building is itself an attraction.

**Skeptic's note:** The hypothesis is unproven. Validate with: (a) full competitor audits, (b) customer interviews, (c) a tasting-event pop-up with a licensed collaboration brewery before committing to build-out.
