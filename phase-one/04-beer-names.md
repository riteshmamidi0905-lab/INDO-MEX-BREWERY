Format: name · pronunciation · language · literal · cultural meaning · style · flavor concept · brand story · legal concern · packaging concept. All beer details are targets `[TEST]`.

| **BARSAAT** | Witbier-like wheat with coriander | Monsoon arrival | Pronunciation hurdle |

**Recommendation:** use BADSHAH only if trademark and cultural review pass; the stronger long-term naming system is *place + element* (DECCAN SOL, GOA BAJA) for the Regional series and *single resonant words* (SONA, ANGARA, HAWA) for the Classics.
