The Court

A brewery you walk into.

A court you walk out to. Limestone, glass, shade and long tables.

The route

From the street to the court.

The Court Brewhouse is envisioned as a place you move through: compressed, then released. It is built from limestone, steel, cedar and shade, with a brewery you can see and a court you can sit in.

The seven moments

01

Street

A quiet front of pale limestone and glass. One tank glowing behind the window.

02

Threshold

A covered entrance lowers the ceiling and cools the air.

03

Compression

A short, warm passage along the brewery glass. You smell malt and woodsmoke before you see anything.

04

Reveal

Then it opens: sky, shade, an oak, a thin channel of water and the hum of the room.

05

The brewery

Steel tanks behind glass, beside the tables. The brewing is part of the evening.

06

The long tables

Plates in the middle, a beer for the table, a zero-proof drink for everyone.

07

Night

The court glows, warm and never loud.

The reveal

Shade, tables and the tanks beside them.

Inside

A bar, a brewery and a kitchen that share one room.

A long bar with a window into the tanks. The glow of the oven in the arcade wall. Nothing staged: the work is simply visible.

After dark

Warm, not loud.

Low lamps, glowing tanks, a full court. A restaurant courtyard, not a nightclub.

The future Court Brewhouse

A place to arrive, sit down and stay.
