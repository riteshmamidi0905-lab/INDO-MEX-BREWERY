STATUS: research-backed design specification, 2026-10-22. No advisor has reviewed it. ## Reference traditions (DOC = documented, MOD = modern interpretation, SENS = needs a qualified reviewer; sources are search summaries, not opened in full)
Haveli chowk and jharokha/jaali (DOC; functional light and shadow, never a pasted pattern; https://www.studiomatrx.org/guides/vernacular-architecture-rajasthan); Mexican hacienda patio, zaguan (DOC; Nierman, The Hacienda in Mexico, to obtain); Talavera (DOC, SENS: protected denomination, never call anything "Talavera"); Barragan/Legorreta courtyards (MOD: approach, not forms); Company School botanical painting (DOC, SENS: unnamed Indian artists, colonial authorship); Sesse and Mocino expedition (DOC, SENS: credit the artists); Bagru/Ajrakh/Zapotec natural dyeing (DOC, SENS: living crafts; use dye logic only, never "Ajrakh-style").
Botany: marigold (Tagetes erecta; Mexico/Central America native; ritual flower in both cultures: one botanical bloom only, never altar, skull, offering garland; never claim shared ancient tradition) · hibiscus (keep H. rosa-sinensis and H. sabdariffa apart; origin disputed: never label roselle Mexican- or Indian-native) · chile (Mexican-domesticated; draw the plant as botany, not a cartoon pod) · tamarind and mango (route unresolved: no route/date claims) · curry leaf (India; verify range) · maize leaf as the Mexican anchor in place of agave/cactus (repo avoid-list).

## HOODIE 01: THE COURTYARD
Idea: the room with no roof. A plan/elevation line drawing of a courtyard (open void, arcade edge, thresholds), tone-on-tone. Palette: Soot, Limestone, Bone, Indigo; one marigold detail at most. Back: full-back plate, courtyard plan with thresholds (gate / zaguan / chowk) receding; light-and-shadow bands only where a jaali would cast them. Front: small left-chest plate (a single arch outline), no wordmark or logo (D-66). Sleeve: rib-cuff hit with a threshold tick pattern. Garment options to specify with a mill sample: 380-450 GSM heavyweight, brushed-back fleece vs unbrushed French terry (pilling vs drape), garment dye vs piece dye; embroidery for small details (digitising proof), water-based screen print for the back plate; flat cotton drawcord, double-layer hood, side-seam pockets; recycled tissue packaging, care card "Inspired by, not made by or endorsed by", blank neck label.

## Anti-copying and cliche rules
Reverse-image and marketplace sweep with a similarity log before approval; never trace or lightly redraw a historical plate, textile, tile or drawing; name no brand as a reference; avoid skulls, altars, deity/Om/swastika imagery, mandalas, paisley filler, bindis, turbans, sombreros, tribal labels, Devanagari or faux scripts, flag colours, chili cartoons, cactus, agave, elephants, maps-as-wallpaper, "ancient shared heritage" captions; never write "traditional" or "authentic" on a fusion graphic; art is commissioned under a written assignment reviewed by counsel (AI use disclosed).
Needs qualified review: cultural advisors (marigold, Talavera-adjacent, dye references, ritual flowers, "Two Worlds" naming); historians (plant routes); botanist (species, origins); IP attorney (similarity, assignment); garment professional (GSM, shrinkage, fastness, puff/embroidery).
