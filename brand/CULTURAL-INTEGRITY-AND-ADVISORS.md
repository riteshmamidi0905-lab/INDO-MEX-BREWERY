## 3. Things that could reasonably mislead guests (fix list)
1. Dish names that borrow a regional name without the identity (Hyderabadi, Malabar, mole, salan).
2. Any "authentic", "traditional" or "ancient" language on fusion dishes.
3. The implication that India and Mexico shared a cuisine historically.
4. "No beef/no pork" read as halal (we make no halal claim).
5. Marigold or "Day of the Dead" imagery used as décor.
6. Visual canon read as engineered or built reality (concept visuals only).
