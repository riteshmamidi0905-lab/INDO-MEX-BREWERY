> HISTORICAL: ANGAN and other candidate names are not the brand. See brand/NASHA-BRAND-STRATEGY.md (D-62).

**Not beer names; these are company/brewery names.** Working project descriptor stays **INDO MEX BREWERY**; ANGAN BREWING CO. is one candidate among 50 and is not privileged (it ranks where the scores put it).

**Method:** 9 criteria scored 1-5 (5 best) by our judgment `[C]`: Memorability, Pronunciation, American appeal, Indian connection, Mexican connection, Beer credibility, Restaurant credibility, National expansion, Visual branding. Plus **TM conflict risk** 1-5 (5 = most crowded; a guess from word commonness, **not** a search). Adjusted score = sum of 9 − 2×(TM risk − 1); max 45.
**Domain/social research:** not possible from this environment `[Unresolved]`. **No trademark search was run `[PRO]`.**

**New concept found in research: the shared line.** The Tropic of Cancer (~23.4°N) passes through India (eight states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram) `[V: Careers360/Unacademy/Vajiram class sources]` and is reported to cross Mexico `[partially verified; confirm on a map]`. A brand built on a **geographic fact** is ownable, non-stereotypical, scalable and neutral to either culture. Names 40-43 explore it.

**Commentary `[C]`:**
- The latitude family (Twenty-Three North / Latitude 23 / Tropic Line / Paralelo 23) scores highest because it is pronounceable, bilingual-neutral, visually simple (a number and a line) and national-scale friendly. Weakness: it is abstract and could be anyone's; it needs the glass, tank and food to carry personality. Also check: tropic-line brands, sports 'Latitude' names, and the exact line positions.
- **Marigold** has a real cross-cultural story (cempasúchil/genda) but is trademark-crowded.
- **Angan** is meaningful and architectural but carries *no Mexican connection*, and it scores accordingly. The architecture thesis does not require the name.
- **Masa & Malt** is the best 'what we make' name but lacks India.
- Pure Hindi or Nahuatl names score badly on pronunciation for American customers.
- No candidate is cleared. Run knockout searches on the top 15 first.
