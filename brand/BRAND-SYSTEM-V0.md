# Brand System v0 (Phase 4 starter): personality, language, visual principles, cultural rules

**Status:** `[CONCEPT]`. **The parent name is NOT selected.** The working project name stays **INDO MEX BREWERY** (a descriptor, not a brand decision; D-09/D-34 context in `docs/decisions/`). No logo exists; no trademark search has been run `[PRO]`. This document sets the rules any final name must live inside. It extends `brand/BRAND-WORLDS.md` (hybrid "Tank Court"), `NAMING-UNIVERSE-V2.md`, `PARENT-BRAND-CANDIDATES.md` and `visuals/VISUAL-DNA.md` (v2.1, canonical visuals).

## 1. Brand idea (internal; not public copy yet)
**Honest beer, specific food, one shared table.** The brewery is a working machine you can see; the kitchen cooks with a stated logic rather than a costume; the place is built for gathering in Texas heat. Culture appears **through people, technique and ingredients**, never as decoration. Do **not** print the historical thesis "two cuisines that already traded fire, fruit and flower" until its claims are VERIFIED (`research/HISTORICAL-CLAIMS-AUDIT.md`, D-25).

## 2. Personality (what it is / is not)
| Trait | Is | Is not |
|---|---|---|
| **Craft-serious** | Precise about beer and food; shows the work | Snobby, jargon-first |
| **Warm** | Generous, social, family-welcoming in the day | Rowdy bar, nightclub |
| **Curious** | Explains why a pairing works; invites questions | Lecturing, museum-voice at the table |
| **Grounded** | Austin, limestone, shade, steel; honest materials | Theme park, "authentic" kitsch |
| **Respectful** | Credits origins; says "we don't know" when we don't | Appropriating, flattening two cultures into one |
| **Confident, not loud** | One strong colour (marigold), plain words | Neon, exclamation marks, puns |

## 3. Voice and language
- **Plain, specific, present tense.** "Brewed here. Poured at 5 °C." beats "crafted with passion".
- **Say the ingredient and its job.** "Toasted sesame for aroma", not "an exotic twist".
- **Dishes keep their real names** (chicken 65, birria, dal makhani); we describe the method, not the exotic origin.
- **No "authentic", "exotic", "ancient secret", "fusion" as a boast.** Use "inspired by" and name the dishes' sources when it helps.
- **Two languages only where verified.** Spanish, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil or other scripts appear only where contextually right and professionally checked `[PRO]`; never as decoration or faux-script type.
- **History claims** only in the wording approved in the audit (e.g., "Chile peppers reached India in the 1500s, carried by Portuguese trade"; "Marigolds are sacred offerings in both India and Mexico; the flower is American in origin, and how it reached India is still debated"). Everything else waits for scholarship.
- **Examples:** "Six tanks, one brewhouse, brewed this week." · "Two-Pour Hefeweizen: pour, rest, swirl, finish." · "Heat 2 of 3. The raita-crema cools it."
- **Avoid:** costume vocabulary (sombrero, turban, maharaja, fiesta), "spice route" clichés, mocking accents, puns on either culture's words.

## 4. Naming rules
1. **Parent brand:** chosen in Phase 4 from `PARENT-BRAND-CANDIDATES.md` (top candidates by our own scoring: Twenty-Three North, Tropic Line, Paralelo 23, Latitude 23, Marigold, Masa & Malt, among 50). **Required before commitment:** USPTO/state/TTB-COLA/TABC search, domain and social checks, mispronunciation test, cultural-advisor review, translation check `[PRO]`. The shared geographic line (Tropic of Cancer) is an idea, still `[partially verified]`.
2. **Beer names:** plain English style names first (Hefeweizen, Mexican-Style Lager, Vienna Amber); working names (SONA, HAWA, ANGARA) are internal and stay provisional. Culture-pun names are rejected.
3. **Space names:** Tank Cloister, Shade Court, Adventure Court, Game Hall, the Outlet: descriptive and neutral.
4. **Dish names:** the real dish name plus a plain descriptor.
5. A name must work if the architecture changes (D-11 principle: the parent name need not equal the architecture idea).

## 5. Visual principles (see `visuals/VISUAL-DNA.md` for the rules and the Locked DNA for geometry)
| Principle | Rule |
|---|---|
| **One colour** | **Marigold (~#E8870E)** used narrowly: tank sleeves (removable powder-coated, never paint on stainless, D-28), tap-handle rings, a can band, tile stripe, napkin stripe, apron stitch. Never terracotta |
| **Material palette** | Pale limestone, board-formed concrete, blackened steel, polished stainless, copper (real brewing metal), pecan/cedar elm. No brass fixtures, no mosaic tile |
| **Graphic mark** | Ring-in-square family (echoing the tank sleeve and the glass collar); not a deity, mandala or flag |
| **Type** | One contemporary grotesque sans plus a restrained serif; no faux-Devanagari, western-saloon or "ethnic" fonts |
| **Light** | Warm 2200-2400K, shielded, low; fire visible in the kitchen; no string lights/neon |
| **Photography** | Real brewing, real fire, natural grain; people candid and mixed; no costumes; concept visuals labelled "Concept visual: not an operating business" |
| **Packaging (later)** | Bands and patterns, no readable text in generated art; plain English style names |
| **The collar glass** | Raised clear ring ~one-quarter up; the object guests keep |
The Phase One look (terracotta, brass, geometric tile, agave, lattice) is **superseded**. ARC-01-D is the canonical exterior; ARC-02-A is a night-mood reference only (D-47).

## 6. Cultural rules (non-negotiable)
1. **No sacred decoration**: no deity statues, altars, photo-booth shrines, religious iconography on beer or merchandise.
2. **No costumes or props** that stand in for people: sombreros, turbans, saris, mariachi/maharaja costume nights.
3. **No stereotype monuments**: Taj Mahal, Aztec calendar, elephants, mandalas, Day of the Dead props as decor.
4. **Acknowledge the hard parts once, plainly**: Spanish and Portuguese/British colonial history, the human trade on the galleons (Seijas: at least ~8,100 enslaved people from Asia incl. the Indian subcontinent, 1565-1700, per search extract `S-106`) belong in the Crossings gallery with dignity, not as décor or romance.
5. **Living communities**: apong, tesgüino and similar traditions are named only with community consultation.
6. **Advisors before launch** `[PRO]`: at least two Indian-heritage and two Mexican-heritage culinary/cultural advisors review names, imagery, dish descriptions and the history cases (open question OQ-7).
7. **Credit and hire**: credit the traditions the food comes from; hire and promote from both communities; do not ask staff to perform culture.
8. **Respect for ingredients**: marigold is a **colour** choice; we do not claim the flower links the two cultures beyond the parallel the audit allows.
9. **Verification** before any public text, signage, menu or packaging (`research/HISTORICAL-CLAIMS-AUDIT.md`).

Parent brand (TBD) → brewery and restaurant as one place → beer line (Classics / Regional / Reserve) → experiences (Tank Cloister, Shade Court, Adventure Court, Game Hall, Outlet) → merchandise (collar glass, sauces, prints). The descriptor "INDO MEX BREWERY" may stay as a *what-it-is* line under a different parent name, or be dropped (OQ-5 is still the founder's call).
