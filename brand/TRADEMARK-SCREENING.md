## 2. Workflow to a defensible decision (owner: attorney + founder)
1. **Knockout search** (attorney): USPTO (word and design, similar sound/meaning), Texas Secretary of State, common-law use (web, TABC label/permit names, COLA/brewery lists), state and federal. Classes to cover: beer (32), restaurant/bar services (43), retail (35), glassware/apparel (21/25), possibly alcohol-related 33.
2. **Risk ranking:** per name, list exact conflicts, similar marks, goods/services overlap, strength of the mark (descriptive vs distinctive), and geographic scope.
3. **Practical checks:** domain and key social handles (founder or attorney), pronunciation test with ~20 Austinites, translation/meaning check in Spanish, Hindi and Telugu, cultural-advisor review (`brand/CULTURAL-INTEGRITY-AND-ADVISORS.md`).
4. **Decision framework (fixed before results):** eliminate any name with a confusingly similar registered/pending mark in classes 32 or 43; down-rank crowded patterns; prefer distinctive over descriptive; require domain + handle availability or a workable variant; require advisor no-objection. Choose the top name that survives, then file after attorney advice.
5. **Do not commission a logo, signage, packaging or domain purchases for a name until step 4 completes.**
