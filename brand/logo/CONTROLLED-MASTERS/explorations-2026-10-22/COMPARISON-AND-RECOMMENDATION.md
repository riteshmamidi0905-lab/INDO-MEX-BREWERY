# NASHA logo explorations — comparison and provisional recommendation

Status: **EXPLORATION (D-66 gate 1). Not official, not cleared, not final.**
No trademark or clearance search has been done. The working name NASHA is itself
pending legal clearance (D-79/D-80). Nothing here is approved for publication.
The logo is **not finalized**; that decision belongs to Ritesh.

Generator: `brand/tools/logo_explorations.py` (procedural SVG; re-runnable).
Every direction ships wordmark, symbol, monogram, horizontal, stacked and seal
lockups in colour / black / white (flat, no gradients or photo effects), plus an
application board (entrance sign, can labels, hoodie, website hero strip).

| | A "The Courtyard" | B "Two Worlds" | C "NASHA After Dark" |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | Arch/doorway geometry; courtyard as the meeting place | Italic serif with hibiscus-leaf crossbars; two cuisines joined | Heavy stencil cut by an ember "seam"; night-time bar |
| Originality | Medium-high (geometric arch alphabet is custom) | High concept, but italic serif + leaf is a common craft-beer trope | Medium (stencil+cut is a known device) |
| Legibility | High | Medium-high (leaf crossbars read as slashes at small size) | Medium (stencil S is the weakest letter) |
| Scalability | High: symbol and monogram hold at 16 px | Medium: leaf detail lost below ~24 px | High for symbol/monogram; wordmark needs min width |
| Cultural sensitivity | Low risk: abstract architecture, no borrowed motifs | Moderate: floral motif must be reviewed (cultural-authenticity-auditor) | Low risk; avoid any "exotic nightlife" copy |
| Commercial usability | Strong: embosses, etches, signs, one-colour | Fair: needs simplified small-use version | Strong on dark packaging; weak on light stock |

## Provisional recommendation
Direction **A, The Courtyard**, as the lead for continued exploration, with B's
leaf kept only as an optional secondary graphic element, and C's dark palette
as a possible night-mode treatment. This is a recommendation for discussion.

## Known weaknesses (honest)
- B: leaf crossbars still read as slashes near the A legs; needs another pass.
- C: S glyph is a stroked construction, not a refined letterform.
- Fonts: Instrument Serif (B) is OFL; licence record is still required before any use.
- Outlines are procedural, not drawn by a type designer; a professional refinement is advisable before any trademark filing.
