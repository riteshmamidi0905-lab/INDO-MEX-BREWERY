## Honest assessment
- Strength: legible down to ~44 px wordmark / 16 px symbol; one stroke weight; works in one colour.
- Weakness: originality is unverified (geometric arch alphabets exist in the wild); no professional kerning/optical review; S remains the least refined letter.
- The 16 px symbol is close to the limit (arches fuse); treat 24 px as the practical floor for the symbol until tested in print.
- Mockups (signage, cans, glass, hoodie, menu, website) are flat vector concepts. **No photorealistic imagery exists yet.**
