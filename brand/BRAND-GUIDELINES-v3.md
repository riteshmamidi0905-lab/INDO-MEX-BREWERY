Foundation: Direction A "The Courtyard" (refined, `brand/logo/CONTROLLED-MASTERS/courtyard-refined-2026-10-22/`). D-66 stays at EXPLORATION; no trademark search; Ritesh has not finalized anything. Visual atmosphere borrowed from his ChatGPT reference board (mood and palette only).

## Palette
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary ground | Deep forest | `#12372A` |
| Metal accent | Antique gold | `#B8923A` (light: `#D5B565`) |
| Light ground | Warm ivory | `#F4EDDC` |
| Warm accent | Terracotta | `#B8552F` |
| Dark ground / text | Dark charcoal | `#2A2623` |
| Support | Terracotta deep `#9E4524` (any panel carrying small ivory text; ivory/terracotta-deep 5.4) · Forest deep `#0C261D` · Leaf `#4F7A3A` · Saffron `#DB8B2E` (AMBA HAZE accent only) | |

Measured contrast (WCAG 2.x, computed by `brand_v3_art.contrast`): ivory/forest 11.21 · forest/ivory 11.21 · ivory/charcoal 12.85 · gold/charcoal 5.15 · **gold/forest 4.49** (large text, graphics and logos only; use light gold `#D5B565` for small text on forest) · **terracotta/ivory 4.11** (large text/graphics only; never for body copy). **Ivory text on plain terracotta `#B8552F` = 4.11 FAILS for small text** (axe caught it on the website preview) — use Terracotta deep for small text panels.

Use: forest + ivory carry ~70% of any layout; gold is trim, rules and the symbol; terracotta is a single accent per layout; charcoal for night/dark grounds. No gradients on logos.

## Typography
- Wordmark/symbols: custom drawn vectors, never retyped. Use the **small cut** (heavier, looser) ≤ 96 px wide; the two-arch symbol ≤ 32 px.
- Headings/display: Instrument Serif (regular and italic, SIL OFL). Body/UI/labels: Inter (SIL OFL). The reference board used a Playfair-like serif; not adopted (a different face and licence record would be needed).
- Licence records for both fonts must be filed before any publication.

## Secondary symbol
Arch-and-botanical crest (arch outline + three-leaf sprig). Supports merchandise, packaging seals, menus and website accents. Never replaces the wordmark or the three-arch symbol; minimum 24 px.

## Beverage family rules
- Shared: NASHA header lockup at the same position/scale, same grid, same placeholder footer block, forest/ivory/gold family.
- BADSHAH (hefeweizen): forest + antique gold; symmetrical gateway arches, wheat ear, lattice trim (geometric only; no portrait, no crown, no historical figure).
- AMBA HAZE (mango-forward juicy IPA, working concept): ivory + terracotta + saffron + leaf greens; mango and long leaves; italic serif name.
- Only verified specs appear: BADSHAH = hefeweizen; AMBA HAZE = mango-forward juicy IPA concept. ABV, volume, ingredients, certifications, regulatory text and barcode are placeholders.

## Misuse
No stretch, outline, shadow, gradient or photo effects; no AI-generated logos or text; no recolour outside the palette; no use on busy backgrounds without a solid panel; no ™/® symbols.
