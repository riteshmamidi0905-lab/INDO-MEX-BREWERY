**Status:** `[CONCEPT]` + `[ASSUMPTION]`. A skeleton, **not a financial model and not advice**. No proforma exists. All figures are **ranges derived from cited secondary sources** (`research/SOURCES.md` S-76..S-99), most of them search-extract level (the Phase-2 research pass could not open primary pages; re-verify before external use). ## 1. Concept positioning (hypothesis, not validated)
A **destination brewpub**: wheat-and-lager-first beers, a kitchen with a stated cooking logic (India × Mexico), a visible brewhouse in a glazed hall, a shade-engineered court, and (phased) a family landscape, a small game hall and a flagship shop. Decision D-08: **brewpub first, keep canning/distribution possible.** The Austin market evidence supports "differentiate or die": Texas breweries fell 440→420 (2024-25), 22 openings vs 29 closures in 2024, Austin closures continue in 2026 (`S-83, S-82`); lenders are cautious. Brewpubs held up better than microbreweries nationally (brewpub output ≈ -1.7% vs micros ≈ -8.9% in 2025, one account; `S-77`). Whitespace hypotheses (from `research/AUSTIN-COMPETITIVE-LANDSCAPE.md`) need customer validation: a cuisine-led brewpub, architecture as attraction, a wheat-lager-first house, vegetarian strength, climate-engineered outdoor space.

## 5. Brewery capacity scenarios (derived)
Locked-canon fermenters (5 ft × 19 ft) are **~63 bbl gross (~50 working) each**, i.e. production-brewery scale (`MASTER-PLAN-PHASE2.md` §5). Annual output = 6 fermenters × working volume × turns × utilisation.
| Scenario | Fermenter (gross / working) | Ideal bbl/yr (12 / 20 / 26 turns) | At ~65% utilisation (A-5) | Fits demand (~780 bbl at 300 seats, `S-76`)? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A: 15 bbl** | ~15 / ~12 | 860 / 1,440 / 1,870 | ~560-1,220 | **Yes** (baseline hypothesis) |
| **B: 30 bbl** | ~30 / ~24 | 1,730 / 2,880 / 3,740 | ~1,120-2,430 | Only with packaging/self-distribution/events |
| **C: 60 bbl (visual canon)** | ~60 / ~48 | 3,460 / 5,760 / 7,490 | ~2,250-4,870 | **No**, unless a packaging/wholesale business is intended |
Texas BP production cap is 10,000 bbl per location and self-distribution to retailers is capped at 1,000 bbl/location (2,500 per licensee) with conditions (**tier T1**, official-domain summaries, not opened; `research/VERIFICATION-LEDGER.md` V-01/V-02; attorney to confirm; §13). A 780-bbl on-site demand is a **taproom-median extrapolation**, not a forecast; a restaurant-led destination may drink more or less. **Decision (D-51):** feasibility baseline = Scenario A/B; the visual canon stays illustrative.

## 12. Revenue streams and logic
| Stream | Driver tree | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **On-premise beer** | seats × turns × pints per cover × price; ≈ 2.6 bbl/seat/yr taproom median (`S-76`) | Weak for a restaurant-led site |
| **Food** | covers × check; food share of taproom/pub sales ~15% (2021) → >20% (recent) (`S-78`), a **40% food** vendor benchmark for brewpubs (`S-84`, C) | Our food-led concept may exceed 40-50% |
| **To-go / crowlers / cans** | on-site package attach rate; brewpubs may sell own beer off-premise in ≤ ½ barrel containers (`S-91`) | Licensing: attorney |
| **Retail (Outlet)** | traffic × conversion × basket; **no merch share evidence** (BA/Arryved reports merch and retail beer falling per taproom) | Treat as small until measured |
| **Events and private hire** | rooms × bookings × minimum spend | Unresolved |
| **Family/arcade/play** | admissions, pay-per-play, parties | Unresolved; one uncorroborated party range ($350-$700) |
| **Brewery experiences** | tours, classes, Brewer's Table | Unresolved |
| **Sponsorship/collaboration** | Celis collab, artists, chefs | Opportunistic |
| **Future packaged beer** | self-distribution ≤ 1,000 bbl/location (`S-90`) or a separate manufacturer structure | Attorney |
Logic: **on-site revenue per bbl is ~231% higher than distributed** (BA benchmark, `S-76`): supports destination-first and caution about packaged-volume dreams.

## 13. Licensing questions for the attorney (no conclusions)
1. BP on BG vs MB: which underlying permit suits a restaurant-led concept (mixed drinks? food-revenue rules)?
2. Guest beer under BP/BG: allowed? tied-house limits? (inferred yes; **unresolved**)
3. Self-distribution mechanics (§74.08 conditions, territorial agreement mention) and whether a brewpub and a manufacturer-tier permit can sit in one entity; future canning plant structure.
4. Minors and an arcade/play area on a licensed premises; premises diagram; local-option rules.
5. Packaged sauces and merchandise (DSHS Food Manufacturer licence; cottage food does not fit).
6. Austin zoning use class for brewpub/brewery and outdoor entertainment; impervious cover for the chosen site; sound limits (85 dB venue / 70 dB Restaurant (General) per search extracts; 100 ft residential setback) (`S-95, S-96`).
7. Austin Water General Industrial User permit and pretreatment plan (`S-94`); early plan review.
8. TTB formula approval for kokum/tamarind/hibiscus/mango/chile beers (`S-109`).
9. 2025-26 legislative changes (89th session update, `S-98`; SB 1184 status unknown) and Austin's land-code rewrite.
