# Guest Experience Architecture v1

> HISTORICAL (Track A concept): not evidence and not scoped to the Core 5 development program.

**Status:** `[PROPOSED]` experience design for a future venue. **No guest preference is asserted**: audiences are planning archetypes, not research findings; the interview instrument (`research/customer-discovery/`) is how we test them. Spatial facts: `visuals/LOCKED-SCENE-DNA.md`, `architecture/SPATIAL-VISION-V1.md`. The earlier 14-stage physical journey stays in `GUEST-JOURNEY-V1.md`; this document adds the **full lifecycle** and **audience variants**. Technology items are proposals and carry cost/privacy questions.

## 1. Lifecycle (Discovery → Return) and what we design at each step
| Stage | Design intent **[Proposed]** | Key design elements | Risk / open question |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | A guest understands the idea in one image and one sentence | Hero image (ARC-01-D class), plain concept line, pairing story, menu preview, map/hours; word of mouth via the brewery sight | Reputation unproven; website not built |
| **Arrival** | Easy, shaded, obvious | Drive reveal of the glass hall, covered approach, door host, rideshare zone | Heat, parking, wayfinding (split shop/eat/play) |
| **Entry** | Threshold that says "made here" | Entrance recess, optional Two Worlds threshold, History Corridor (optional), first sight of the Cloister | Queue in sun; forced gallery for families |
| **Seating** | Fast, fair, party-aware | Host sees bar/dining/court/terrace capacity; seating by party type; QR waitlist (proposal) | Wait time; walk-aways |
| **Ordering** | Low friction, never confusing | Table/bar ordering model undecided (counter vs server vs QR); pairing cards; heat scale 0-3 | Service model unresolved; tech cost |
| **Beer discovery** | Approachable for non-experts, rich for enthusiasts | Tap board with plain styles, flight options, taste-before-you-buy, beer guide | Austin hop-forward preference (T3) |
| **Food** | Recognisable, shareable, paced | Shareables first, tacos/birria, vegetarian pillar, tadka at the table | Ticket times, kitchen capacity (untested) |
| **Exploration** | Reasons to walk around | Cloister view, mezzanine, court, rill, art, optional history cases, later play | Over-curation |
| **Social interaction** | Places for 2, 4, 12 | Bar, communal pecan tables, court, private room, stage alcove | Noise zoning |
| **Payment** | Frictionless, splittable | Pay at table/bar; tip policy `[Founder]`; split checks | POS and fees TBD |
| **Departure** | A take-home moment | Glass/can/sauce/print at the Outlet, safe departure, ride-share | To-go law (ledger V-03), retail demand |
| **Return** | A reason to come back | Rotating seasonal beer, brew calendar, regional nights (not costume nights), membership (if legal/viable), journal updates | Loyalty value unproven |

## 2. Audience journeys (archetypes **[Proposed]**; validate by interview)
| Audience | Arrives wanting | Path through the venue | Moments that must work | Likely friction | What to test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Couple / date night** | Atmosphere, a shared first-time dish, good light | Evening arrival → Cloister glow → bar for a flight → shared plates in the court or dining → dessert | Warm lighting, a conversation-friendly table, paired flight | Noise, wait times | Interview "who would you bring"; evening observation at pop-up |
| **Group of friends** | Room for a table, easy ordering, shareables | Court communal table → shareables + pitcher/flight → games later | Shareable "wow" dish (Chicken 65 Totopos), large-table service | Splitting bills, big-group seating | Group seating and splitting test at pop-up |
| **Family** | Shade, quick seating, kids' food, safety | Side door to court → family terrace → kids' menu → play (later) → Outlet | Fast seating, clear heat levels, safe play, nearby restrooms | Heat, noise, minors policy | Family interviews; legal/insurance review |
| **Beer enthusiast** | Brewer access, quality, rare pours | Corridor → Cloister view → flight → brewer's table | Posted brew event, brewer visible, reserve taps | Quality below Hefeweizen standard | Panel results (none yet) |
| **Food enthusiast** | Technique, specificity, vegetarian depth | Open kitchen view → tadka at table → pairing card → dessert | Kitchen theatre, honest dish notes | Execution consistency | T1 results (none yet) |
| **First-time visitor** | To understand the place quickly | Door host → short orientation line → pairing cards → flight | One-sentence concept, host script, explained menu | Information overload | Comprehension questions in interviews |
| **Tourist** | A memorable Austin experience, photogenic | Photo moment at the Cloister → flight → shared plates → glass to take home | Hero photo spot, easy ordering, take-home object | Findability, parking | Observation; destination data later |
| **Repeat local guest** | Reliability and something new | Bar seat → seasonal beer → new special → brewery news | Rotating items, staff recognition, brew calendar | Staleness | Repeat-visit questions in interviews |

## 3. Key experience moments **[Proposed designs]**
| Moment | Design intent | Delivery mechanism | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arrival** | Warm, shaded, obvious | Glass hall glow, canopy, door host | Site, architect |
| **Brewery visibility** | Honest: real work guests can see | Glass wall, posted brew event, brewer present | Real vessel size, brew schedule |
| **Menu discovery** | Understand the idea in 60 seconds | One-page pairing menu, heat scale, "our own dish" labels | Tested menu |
| **Beer-flight decision** | Easy for non-experts | Pre-set flights with 3-line stories | Beer pilots pass gates |
| **Aroma/food theatre** | Sound/smell/movement | Tadka at the table, tortilla griddle, tandoor visible | Safety and hood design (PRO) |
| **Tandoor visibility** | Fire as the kitchen's signature | Open pass, visible tandoor mouth | Hood/fire code (PRO) |
| **Family experience** | Parents relax, kids play safely | Terrace sightlines, Adventure Court (later) | G-9, insurance, CPSI |
| **Photo moment** | Naturally shareable, not staged | Cloister view, collar glass in light, court at dusk | Visual identity, lighting |
| **Merchandise** | "I want that glass" | Outlet Glass Wall | Glass manufacturing path, retail law |
| **Takeaway** | A reason to continue at home | Crowlers/cans if legal, sauces if licensed, print | G-11, DSHS |
| **Loyalty/revisit** | Rewarded curiosity | Seasonal tap rotation, brew calendar, membership (if viable) | Legal/economic viability |

## 4. Technology proposals (all optional, all unvalidated)
QR menu with allergen/dietary filters and pairing explanations; waitlist text; split-pay; brew-schedule display; brewery-sensor "tank now" display only if it is truthful and maintained; loyalty/membership. **Principles:** accessible, low-friction, privacy-respecting, never replace hospitality; costs and data policy TBD.

## 5. Accessibility and inclusion
Step-free routes on every audience path `[PRO ADA]`; sensory-friendly options (quiet hours, seating away from stage); dietary clarity (vegetarian, allergens, no beef/pork; **no halal claim**); language: English, with other languages only if verified.

## 6. Measurement plan (pop-up stage)
Time to first beer; wait quality; first-time comprehension (can they say what the place is?); shareable-dish selection; flight attach; photo moments counted by observation; family dwell; 3-question exit card. **No numbers exist.**
