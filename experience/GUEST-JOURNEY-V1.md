## 1. The journey in one line
*You see a lit glass hall of tanks from the drive → you walk a short quiet corridor and the six-tank Cloister opens in front of you → you take a seat at a bar that looks into it → you eat food whose logic you can taste and a beer that is brewed thirty feet away → you drift into a shaded court → you leave carrying a glass.*

## 2. Stage-by-stage design
| # | Stage | Guest goal | What they see / hear / smell | Where (Locked DNA) | Staff touchpoint | Main risk | What we measure (pilot/pop-up) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Parking and approach** | Park easily, know where to go | Low, subordinate parking (S and SW edges); tank hall glowing/clear glass visible from the drive (the ARC-01-D view); live oaks, arcade lights | South entry drive; covered approach under the south arcade | None; wayfinding by light and shade, one blank sign panel | Heat on asphalt, long walk, confusing split (shop vs. restaurant vs. play) | Walk time car→door ≤ 2-3 min; % of arrivals who find entrance without help |
| 2 | **Arrival / threshold** | Feel welcomed, orient | Covered 12×12 ft entrance recess; cooler air from the arcade; faint grain/wood-fire smell, never wort-boil smell at the door | Entrance recess beside the hall's SE corner | **Door host/greeter** at the recess (shade, queue management) | Queue in sun; wrong door for families or shoppers | Max outdoor queue time; heat-index trigger for indoor holding |
| 3 | **Two Worlds threshold** | Pause, understand the premise | Dark, quiet room, one photograph, one sentence; no flag/map/costume | Lobby, behind the recess side wall | None | Overloading it with story; blocking flow | Dwell < 20 s; no queue forms here |

## 3. Paths (personas, not demographics)
| Path | Who | Route | Design implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. First-time beer-curious couple** | Wants the "wow" and a good pour | Drive reveal → corridor → Cloister → bar → dining | Corridor and Cloister reveal must be flawless |
| **B. Family with young children** | Needs fast seating, shade, safe play | Entrance → host → court/terrace table → Adventure Court; skips corridor via side door | Family fast path; terrace sightlines; kids' menu fast |
| **C. Beer enthusiast** | Wants brewer access and rare pours | Corridor → Cloister → bar flight → brewer's table | Posted brew schedule; reserve taps |
| **D. Shopper** | Wants the glass or sauces | South arcade → Outlet; maybe a beer afterward | Outlet door; separate hours |
| **E. Event/group** | 20-80 people | Private route → events room → court | Back-of-house kept separate |
| **F. Accessible path** | Mobility needs | Step-free on every route `[PRO ADA]` | No stage forces a step; elevator to mezzanine |

## 4. The memorable moments (hypotheses to test)
1. **The drive reveal**: from the road, a glass hall full of polished tanks glowing in blue hour (ARC-01-D day; night is still to be solved visually).
2. **The Cloister reveal at the promenade**: six tanks, copper deck, the brewer at work. Needs one real event per day.
3. **Two-Pour Hefeweizen** in the ring-collar glass: the pour and the collar are photographable, repeatable and teachable.
4. **The tadka at the table**: ghee-curry leaf tadka poured over the birria or dal-frijol (sound, smell, movement) `[TEST: safety, ops]`.
5. **The Chaat Elote / Chicken 65 Totopos** as the shareable "I've never had this" snack.
6. **The shade that works**: guests say it is cooler in the court at 4 pm in July; this is the credibility test.
7. **A kid's day-win**: an Adventure Court that feels like landscape, not plastic.
8. **The glass to take home**: ring-collar glass in the Outlet's Glass Wall.
9. **The brew calendar**: "knock-out at 6:30"; the room is actually alive (Honesty rule, `architecture/COURT-BREWHOUSE-V2.md`).
Test each by simple observation and a 3-question exit card (see section 7).

## 5. Weather and day-part variants
| Condition | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| **Hot (≥ 100°F; ~33 days/yr average, 69 in 2023 `[V S-40]`)** | Indoor-first seating, fans and rill on, Adventure Court shaded and morning/evening shifted, water stations, misting only if validated `[PRO]` |
| **Rain/thunderstorm** | Covered arcade edge keeps the court partly usable; roll-down panels `[PRO]`; Adventure Court closed on lightning |
| **Winter evenings** | Radiant heaters, fire tables (code review), glass wind screens, dark beers feature |
| **Weekday lunch** | Bar and dining; fast tacos; no stage |
| **Weekend daytime** | Family hours; Adventure Court and Game Hall peak |
| **Evenings (after ~8 pm)** | Adults-focused; Game Hall and Adventure Court close earlier than the bar (policy, `[PRO]` minors rules); music at the stage |
| **Events** | Private route; stage soundproofing |

## 6. Friction register (what can ruin the journey)
Heat on arrival; queue in sun; confusing first decision (shop / eat / play); loud stage near tables; slow first beer; glass shortage; long ticket times; unsafe play surfaces; unclear minors/alcohol policy; no brewing activity at 2 pm; parking overflow; rideshare chaos. Each has an owner and a metric in the operating plan (Phase 3).

## 7. How to test the journey before building
1. **Cardboard walkthrough:** floor-plan the footprint on a field with tape and string; run 6-8 people through paths A-F.
2. **Pop-up:** two-to-four events serving 6-10 dishes and 4-6 beers; measure time to first beer, ticket times, queue, 3-question exit card (what would you tell a friend; what surprised you; what would you change).
3. **Heat test:** measure surface temperatures and thermal comfort in the Austin summer at a rented shaded site `[TEST/PRO]`.
4. **Playground and arcade operator interviews** (including a CPSI) `[PRO]`.

## 8. Decisions made now (reversible)
| ID | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| J-1 | The History Corridor is **optional**; families have a side door to the court | Hungry children should not be forced through a gallery |
| J-2 | The Outlet has its own door and hours | Non-diners can shop; avoids forced walk-through |
| J-3 | Both reveals stay: the drive reveal (exterior) and the Cloister reveal (corridor) | Each earns a distinct memory |
| J-4 | One real brewing event is posted daily | Honesty rule; keeps the room alive |
| J-5 | Evening policy: Adventure Court and Game Hall close before the bar | Adult-evening protection; pending legal check |
| J-6 | Journey metrics are collected at pop-up stage, not invented | No made-up conversion figures |
