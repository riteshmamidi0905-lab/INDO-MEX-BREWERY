Why NASHA

Two food cultures in conversation, grounded in Austin.

Indian and Mexican kitchens already speak a similar language: chile, lime, toasted spice, flatbread, fermentation and fire. NASHA brings the two into conversation through food, beer and hospitality, in a city that loves both.

It is a founder's vision, and it is being developed deliberately before it opens.

The philosophy

Food and beer designed together to draw you in.

Texan bones. Non-Texan details.

The bones are Texan: limestone, live oaks, shade, gravel and a long table. The details come from the food, the glass and the objects on the table.

The place

A court you can sit in.

About this preview

A concept in development.

This is the founder's vision for NASHA, shared privately. The pictures are concept visualizations made with AI tools: no dish has been cooked, no beer brewed, no building built, and the people shown are not real guests. NASHA is a working name, and the name and the cultural details will be reviewed with the right advisors before anything is made public.

The word nasha means intoxication or addiction in Hindi and Urdu. It was chosen for the feeling of being drawn in by food, craft and company, and the project will never present excess as something to admire.

Founder: Ritesh Mamidi. Austin, Texas.
