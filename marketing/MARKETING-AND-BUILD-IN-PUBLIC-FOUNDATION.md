## 3. Build-in-public (the idea and its guardrails)
**Idea:** documenting the real creation of an Indo-Mex brewery could itself be a marketing and investor-confidence asset: "Building an Indo-Mex brewery from scratch". **Content that is honest and valuable:** menu experiments, dish failures, beer development (once real), design decisions, cultural-advisor conversations (with consent), food-truck/pop-up development, finding a location, the investor journey (carefully), construction later, opening countdown.
**Guardrails:** (1) **Only real progress**: no staged milestones, no invented tests, no "launching soon" without a basis. (2) **Label status** (concept / testing / real). (3) **Do not publish** trade secrets (full recipes, supplier terms), confidential investor assumptions, or advisors/partners without permission. (4) **Cultural sensitivity**: credit sources; do not publish unreviewed history claims. (5) **Legal**: alcohol advertising, health claims, endorsements, and privacy; attorney review of beer content. (6) **IP**: provenance for every asset (`ip/IP-OWNERSHIP-FRAMEWORK.md`). (7) **Sustainability**: a cadence the founder can keep; failure posts are allowed and encouraged. (8) **Investor lens**: public progress is an evidence stream, never a substitute for the data room.
**Hypothesis to test:** that build-in-public raises awareness and trust at low cost. Measure by signups, follows, QR scans, inbound partner/advisor interest; **no results exist**.

## 4. Content pillars (proposed)
Food in progress · the craft of beer (education, no jargon) · cultural curiosity (honest, advisor-reviewed) · Austin places and people · the build (decisions, mistakes, numbers when appropriate) · pairing (what to drink with it).

## 5. Rules and metrics
Every channel has an owner, a purpose, a cadence and a metric before launch; no channel is added "because everyone has one". Core metrics: signups per 100 reach, event conversion, repeat rate, cost per acquired customer (only when paid), press mentions of real milestones. No vanity targets set in advance without a baseline.
