Five industrial-form studies (no ring, subtle single, soft wide, narrow, double-step) generated by `tools/glass_params.py studies visuals/glass-studies` and compared in `collar-studies-montage.png` (`tools/render_ortho.py`, Blender). The rings are plain geometric bands: NOT a logo, mark or text (D-66), NOT a trademark, NOT a production spec. Body profile, 4 mm-scale ring dimensions, 2.2 mm wall, 8 mm base, 400 ml fill and the soda-lime density (2.5 g/cm3) and liquid density (1.01 g/ml) are TEST inputs or assumptions, not NASHA specifications; the body is a barrel-like placeholder, not a Weizen/Lager/Pilsner design. `studies.json` holds brimful capacity (705.8 ml for the test body), glass volume/mass, fill-line height, centre of mass and static tip angle. The ring changes glass volume and mass slightly and does not change capacity. A glass manufacturer must confirm wall, rim, base, annealing, tolerance, stackability and cost. No design-completeness points are claimed.


## Core 5 family (Sprint 16): `core5/`
Five glasses (Hefeweizen, Mexican-Style Lager, Vienna Amber, Pilsner, Witbier) built by `python3 tools/core5_glasses.py build` from concept TARGETS: brimful capacities (680, 470, 415, 430, 400 ml) are HAND ESTIMATES (UNVERIFIED) that the script calibrates rim and belly diameters to, with the base diameter held at its spec value; serving volumes (500, 400, 350, 330, 330 ml) are liquid only (excluding foam); the fill-line height is measured from the outside bottom. Four volumes are never mixed: solid glass volume (cm3), internal brimful capacity, serving volume, fill-line height. Brimful is checked two ways (mesh vs numeric integration of the same inner profile, within 0.3%): that checks the arithmetic, not the design. Known weaknesses: a barrel-like profile that is not style-true (no Weizen waist and flare, no Pilsner taper), no rim radius or fire-polish, flat floor, no grip/stack/nest model, low static tip angles for the tall slim glasses (about 14-15 degrees; a ranking, not a safety figure), the outer-only ring thickens the wall locally, and the rings sit low per GLASSWARE-V2 and read as a shelf rather than a grip band. CONCEPT, NOT ENGINEERED; MANUFACTURABILITY UNVERIFIED; no logo, text or mark (D-66).

## Glass Family V2 (Sprint 17): `v2/` supersedes `core5/`
Built by `python3 tools/glass_v2.py build` from `v2/specs.json` (control points, wall, base, rim and fillet radii, densities: all DESIGN ASSUMPTIONS). Style-specific profiles replace the Sprint 16 barrel: tall Hefeweizen with waist and flare, straight Mexican-lager shape, Vienna bowl, tapered Pilsner, short Witbier. Metrics are exact for the assumed profile (Pappus' theorem and polygon moments) and cross-checked against a trimesh revolve (all within 0.1%, watertight): this checks arithmetic, not the design. Outputs: `glass-v2-summary.csv` (capacity, serving fill, fill-line height, headspace, centre of mass, static tip angle), `glass-v2-silhouettes.png` (side-by-side silhouettes with fill lines and dimensions). Four volumes stay separate: solid glass, internal brimful, serving liquid, headspace. Rim has a 0.6 mm round, base corner 3.5 mm, floor fillet 3.0 mm.
- **Stability:** Hefeweizen filled tips at about 14.8 degrees and is flagged (Pilsner and Mexican lager are within about 1 degree of the marker, 15.9); 15 degrees is a screening marker, not a standard or safety figure. Raising the base radius is the main lever; a manufacturer decides.
- **Collar ring:** computed as a variant (`*-ring.stl`) but OFF by default: it adds roughly 0.3-0.5 cm3 of glass and 0-3 mm of width, and only helps if it improves grip without harming cleaning, stacking and manufacturing. Not a logo, mark or text (D-66).
- **Glass count:** an AI operations-agent view recommended two shapes (tall and short) plus a conditional third for Hefeweizen; an AI designer-agent view kept five (neither is a manufacturer or bar manager). This is an unresolved disagreement, left to the founder and a manufacturer; the geometry supports either.
- **Known remaining weaknesses:** no punt, uniform wall, no draft angle, rim bead untested, thermal-shock base-to-wall ratio about 3.75, rack fit and nesting depth unchecked. Manufacturer questions are in `beer/GLASS-SPECIFICATION-CHECKLIST.md`.
- STL files are git-ignored (regenerable, about 90 MB); the script, specs, CSV and PNG are the source of record. CONCEPT GEOMETRY, MANUFACTURABILITY UNVERIFIED.
