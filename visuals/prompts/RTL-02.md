## MASTER PROMPT

```
Photorealistic professional photograph, specialist architectural/interior photographer, full-frame camera, real materials, believable scale, natural imperfections, neutral true-colour grade, fine grain, no CGI look. FORMAT: 4:5. SCENE: a minimalist retail installation: a wall of blackened-steel niches in a precise grid of 6 columns by 5 rows, each niche holding one empty hero Hefeweizen glass: a tall 0.5-litre wheat-beer vase about 24-25 cm tall with a thick clear base, fine rim and one raised clear glass ring (collar) about one-quarter of the way up from the base. All thirty glasses are identical, perfectly clear, with no print, logo, colour or metal. Each niche is lit from above by a narrow warm beam so the rings catch light and cast soft shadows; the repetition of the rings creates a rhythm; the floor in front is bone terrazzo with a soft reflection; a single softly blurred customer at the far edge reaches toward the wall. Honed limestone surrounds the installation. Nothing else: no signs, no text, no clutter. CAMERA: 50mm, straight on, slight low angle. LIGHTING: Narrow-beam warm 2300K lights above each niche, soft reflections. REALISM: believable quantities, correct physics, natural imperfections.
```
