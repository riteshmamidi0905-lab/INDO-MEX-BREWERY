## MASTER PROMPT

```
Photorealistic professional photograph, specialist architectural/interior photographer, full-frame camera, real materials, believable scale, natural imperfections, neutral true-colour grade, fine grain, no CGI look. FORMAT: 3:2. INTERIOR LANGUAGE (same venue as every other image): matte-polished bone terrazzo floor with sparse red-and-black aggregate; honed pale Texas limestone walls; blackened-steel framing, shelving and rails; dark pecan timber; clear glass in dark-bronze frames; dark acoustic ceilings; warm shielded 2200-2400K light with focused museum-grade accent lighting only where objects are displayed; marigold orange used extremely sparingly (a single stripe, tab, ring or tile); contemporary sans-serif typography only; ALL labels and signs are blank small rectangles with no readable text. SCENE: a cultural design store and brewery shop of about 1,200 sq ft with its own glazed door to a shaded arcade: central dark-pecan display tables with books and folded charcoal and bone apparel, blackened-steel rails with aprons, work shirts and caps, a restrained shelf of small house sauces and spice jars in plain black-and-bone packaging with no text, framed prints on a limestone wall, a blackened-steel refrigerated wall with glass doors holding matte-black cans each with one marigold band (no text) at the back, a minimal lit wall of ring-collar glasses at the left, a small stainless-and-pecan counter with a staff member in a charcoal apron. The mood is gallery-like and uncluttered. PEOPLE: a realistic mix of Austin customers of different ages, backgrounds and styles in ordinary everyday clothes, natural unposed candid behaviour, nobody looking at the camera, small or softly blurred; not grouped by ethnicity; no costumes, no sombreros, no turbans or saris as props; staff in charcoal canvas aprons over bone shirts with a small marigold stitch. A few shoppers browsing naturally. CAMERA: 24mm, eye level. LIGHTING: Daylight from a large window to the shaded arcade plus warm 2300K gallery lighting. REALISM: believable quantities, correct physics, natural imperfections.
```
