## MASTER PROMPT

```
Photorealistic professional photograph, specialist architectural/interior photographer, full-frame camera, real materials, believable scale, natural imperfections, neutral true-colour grade, fine grain, no CGI look. FORMAT: 3:2. INTERIOR LANGUAGE (same venue as every other image): matte-polished bone terrazzo floor with sparse red-and-black aggregate; honed pale Texas limestone walls; blackened-steel framing, shelving and rails; dark pecan timber; clear glass in dark-bronze frames; dark acoustic ceilings; warm shielded 2200-2400K light with focused museum-grade accent lighting only where objects are displayed; marigold orange used extremely sparingly (a single stripe, tab, ring or tile); contemporary sans-serif typography only; ALL labels and signs are blank small rectangles with no readable text. SCENE: an optional hands-on corner at the edge of the play area: a wall of pecan drawers each holding a different grain to touch, a row of glass smell jars (spices, chile, vanilla, cacao, lime) with blank tags, a mechanical table map with sliding wooden markers and thin white route lines (no labels); children and a parent touching and smelling; a brewer showing a bubbling fermenting jar to two children. Everything is natural material; no screens. CAMERA: 35mm, eye level. LIGHTING: Soft daylight and warm light. REALISM: believable quantities, correct physics, natural imperfections.
```
