## MASTER PROMPT (self-contained; paste whole)

```
Photorealistic premium industrial-design collection photograph of four EMPTY drinking glasses. FORMAT: 3:2. Glass design (canonical): this glass belongs to a family of four that share one design feature, a RAISED CLEAR-GLASS RING (collar) moulded integrally into the glass about one-quarter of the way up from the base, about 5 mm proud of the surface and about 12 mm tall with softly rounded edges, never wider than the rim. All glass is perfectly clear, colourless, with no print, no etching, no logo, no pattern, no colour, no metal. THE FOUR GLASSES, left to right, standing on a honed pale Texas limestone slab, evenly spaced about one glass-width apart and very slightly staggered in depth: (1) HEFEWEIZEN, the tallest: about 24 to 25 cm tall, a tall wheat-beer vase, thick clear base, subtle narrowing then widening to a fine rim about 7.8 cm across, ring collar about 6 cm above the base. (2) LAGER: about 20 cm tall, a tall refined conical glass, base about 5 cm, rim about 7.2 cm, thin straight-sided conical walls, ring collar one-quarter of the way up. (3) RESERVE: a short stemmed tasting goblet about 17 cm tall, with a bowl about 8 cm wide featuring ONE subtle step (a gentle shoulder) in the bowl, a slim stem and a stable round foot; the raised ring sits at the junction between the bowl and the stem, echoing the others. (4) FLIGHT: a small tulip tasting glass about 10 cm tall holding about 120 ml (4 oz), gently flared lip, ring collar one-quarter of the way up. The four are clearly one design family because of the shared ring but are not identical; their heights step down from the Hefeweizen. All perfectly clear, empty, spotless, with no print, logo or colour. STUDIO: warm bone-grey seamless background with a very subtle gradient, one large soft raking light from camera-left revealing every ring, rim and base with clean highlights and thin dark edges, gentle fill from the right, soft reflections and faint soft shadows on the stone. CAMERA: 85 to 100mm lens, aperture about f/8, camera at about the height of the Hefeweizen's midpoint, minimal distortion, all four in sharp focus, composed like a professional collection catalogue with generous negative space. Nothing else in frame.
```
