## 3. Rules for this test set
1. **Policy gate:** every recipe, stock, fat, cheese and supplier spec passes the D-54 checklist before cooking (lard-free tortillas and beans; no beef/pork stock; vegetarian rennet question logged).
2. **Identity gate:** each dish states what must remain recognisable to a specialist of each tradition (T1 sheets).
3. **Lamb cut comparison (dish 8):** compare **commercially realistic cuts** at T1 for flavour, tenderness, char, yield, consistency, prep time, supplier reliability, cost and waste. Candidate cuts to put in front of a butcher or supplier include leg (boneless), shoulder, and trimmed loin or rack offcuts; **the winner is not chosen in advance**.
4. **Two proteins, two cooks:** Dum Goat Birria (slow, braised, deep, consommé-driven, dum-inspired) and Tandoori Lamb Taco (charred, grilled, brighter, faster, herb/chile/acid-forward) must taste unmistakably different. The lamb taco is **our own composition** inspired by Punjabi tandoori marination and Sonoran grilling/tortilla logic, **not a historical India-Mexico dish**.
5. **Recording:** weights, times, yields, holding, waste, scores in the T1 sheets; cost fields are filled with supplier quotes only (no invented values).
6. **Panel:** per `MENU-DEVELOPMENT-MATRIX-V1.md` §7 (T2): at least two chefs per cuisine background and cultural advisors `[PRO]`.

## 4. Test sequence (suggested)
Week-style order, no dates promised: (1) policy/supplier screening; (2) sauces (Lal, Hari, Imli-Guajillo, Adobo-Dum, Makhani-Rajas, Raita-Crema, Macha-Tadka, Malabar); (3) the five T1 sheets; (4) the remaining seven wave-1 dishes with the same template; (5) T2 panel with beer pairing; (6) T3 pop-up design (`research/market-survey/`).
