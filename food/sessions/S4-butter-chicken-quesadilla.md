## Day plan
1. T-3 h: marinate chicken (yogurt-spice) at 5 C or below
2. T-2.5 h: char chicken to 74 C core, cool, dice about 1 cm; roast/steam/peel poblanos (30 min), drain and pat dry
3. T-2 h: cook the shared sauce base, weigh, split, reduce A and B to their weight targets; salt after; fold in chicken
4. Chill filling in SHALLOW trays to 5 C or below within 2 h; do not assemble warm
5. T-20 min: preheat dry comal medium; assemble (cheese against both faces, filling centre, rajas against cheese)
6. Griddle, light press, rest 1-2 min; the centre must reach 74 C (probe); cut 3 wedges; plate within 3 min; score at T+0, T+5 (filling integrity), T+10 (crispness, leak); blot leak at T+10 and weigh
