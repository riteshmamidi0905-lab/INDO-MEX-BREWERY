## Day plan
1. Soak guajillo in hot water 20 min, blend with tamarind, sugar, water; reduce the glaze (weigh); make slaw, pickled onion, salsa
2. Blanch florets about 3 min, steam-dry on a rack until the surface is dry; weigh
3. Marinate all florets with the same marinade 30-60 min at 5 C or below
4. Arm A: roast about 200 C on an uncrowded tray; Arm B: batter just before frying at about 175 C in small batches (thermometer in the oil; lid nearby)
5. Glaze to order (weigh grams per taco); plate within 3 min; score at T+0, T+5 (glaze_adhesion), T+10 (crispness, tortilla), record crunch at T+15
6. Cool the oil completely before disposing of it (not down the drain)

## Safety, D-54, allergens
- Hot oil: heavy pot, thermometer, lid or fire blanket nearby, never leave unattended, pot no more than half full.
- Marinated florets at 5 C or below until cooking.

- SOGGY: record panel crunch at T+5 as the evidence (thresholds unchanged).
