## 1. Signature dossier (why each exists)
| Dish | Why it needs to exist | Indian logic | Mexican logic | Identity test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chicken 65 Totopos** | Chicken 65 is already a *bar snack*, eaten with something to scoop; totopos are Mexico's scoop. Same job, two traditions. | Yogurt/ginger/garlic/chile marinated thigh, fried crisp, curry leaf + green chile | Fresh-fried masa totopos, pickled onion, lime, optional cotija-like cheese on the chips not the chicken | A Chennai/Hyderabad eater: "that's 65". A Mexican chef: "proper totopos, balanced" |
| **Dum Goat Birria** | Both cuisines braise goat low and long in a sealed vessel with a chile-based marinade; the connection is **method**, not spice. | Yogurt-papaya pre-marinade, birista-style onion, dum seal, ghee-curry leaf tadka at the table | Guajillo/ancho/pasilla adobo, consommé, griddled tortilla | Birria eaters recognize consommé + tortilla; Indian eaters recognize dum |
| **Butter Chicken Rajas Quesadilla** | Butter chicken's sweet-rich tomato-cream needs **smoke and acid**; charred poblano rajas provide both; the quesadilla gives the sauce a structure instead of a plate. | Tomato-butter-cream, kasuri methi | Flour tortilla (Sonora/Chihuahua), rajas, Oaxaca or Chihuahua cheese | Sauce kept thick, not wet |
| **Gobi Taco** | Fried cauliflower is already a street snack (gobi 65/Manchurian); tacos need crunch + acid. | Spiced batter, tamarind-chile glaze | Corn tortilla, lime slaw, pickled onion | Crisp after 3 minutes |
| **Paneer Tikka Taco** | Tandoor-charred paneer behaves like a taco filling: firm, smoky, absorbs marinade. | Yogurt-ajwain-chile marinade, tandoor char | Corn tortilla, hari salsa, charred pineapple | Charred not rubbery |
| **Malabar Fish Taco** | Kerala fish in pepper-coconut marinade meets the Baja/Yucatán fish taco; both are coastal, acid-forward. | Black pepper, coconut, curry leaf | Grilled or lightly battered fish, cabbage, lime | Fish stays moist |
| **Tandoori Lamb Taco** *(replaces Tandoori Carne Asada, SUPERSEDED by D-54)* | Our own composition: Punjabi tandoori-style marination meets Sonoran-style grilling and the flour tortilla; both cook over live fire with wheat bread. **Not a historical India-Mexico dish.** | Yogurt-chile-ginger marinade | Lamb (cut chosen at test), flour tortilla, onion, salsa | Charred and juicy |
| **Mole-Salan Bowl** | **Salan and mole are the same architecture**: toasted nuts/seeds + dried chile + souring + spice, ground into a thick sauce. Vegetables are the star. | Peanut-sesame-coconut-tamarind salan | Mole technique (toasting, grinding, long simmer) | Neither a curry nor a mole: a third thing |
| **Hefe-brined Wings** | Beer-brined (our hefeweizen) wings tie kitchen to brewhouse; three sauces from the sauce system. | Lal / Imli-Guajillo / Malabar | Salsa verde-style hari | Crisp skin |
| **Chaat Elote** | Roadside corn (Mexico) and bhutta/chaat (India) both use lime, chile, salt, and creamy/crunch layers. | Chaat masala, sev | Mayo/crema, cotija | Exact lime-salt-heat balance |
| **Dal-Frijol Dip** | Black lentils and black beans are both slow-simmered pot staples; the "rolling pot" could echo Pujol's mole madre (time as ingredient). | Dal makhani technique (long simmer, butter), tadka | Beans, epazote, masa chips | Smoky, never thin |

## 4. Reassessed founder concepts (see also `docs/decisions/REJECTED.md`)
| Concept | Verdict | Reasoning and tests |
|---|---|---|
| **Chicken Tikka Masala Quesadilla** | **Reinstated as CONDITIONAL special, differentiated** | Tikka masala and butter chicken sauces are close; the opening menu carries only the butter-chicken version. A *Tikka Quesadilla* is meaningfully different if built dry-charred: yogurt-marinated tandoor chicken, quick onion-pepper masala, Chihuahua cheese, hari salsa, pickled onion (sour-smoky vs. rich-sweet). Test side-by-side in blind tasting; keep only if guests can name the difference. |
| **Tandoori Birria Pizza** | **CONDITIONAL, with kill criteria** | Risks: soggy crust, birria already best in a taco, Texas pizza is crowded (Pinthouse, Aviator `[V S-30, S-28b]`). To pass: drained shredded goat on a high-heat flatbread crust, Oaxaca cheese, charred onion, cilantro-lime, consommé on the side for dipping. Kill if blind tasters rate it lower than the birria taco or if it requires a wet topping. |
| Chilli Chicken Tacos | Parked | Indo-Chinese is a third culture; revisit as a special. |
| Samosa Empanadas / Mole Biryani / Gulab Jamun Tres Leches | Stay rejected | Redundant, muddled or incoherent. |
| Achari Carnitas | **REJECTED (D-54: pork); SUPERSEDED, kept for provenance** | Was: test-kitchen only. A non-pork achari-spice rub may be tested on lamb or chicken, as a new dish, not as carnitas. |
