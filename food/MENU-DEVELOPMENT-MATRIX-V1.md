## 0. How to read the matrix
| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Anchor (people come for it) · Gateway (low-risk first order) · Signature (the dish people talk about) · Margin (protects cost) · Family · Veg (vegetarian anchor) |
| **Regional inspiration** | The real dishes/techniques behind it. Citations: `research/*BENCHMARKS.md`; no history claim beyond `research/HISTORICAL-CLAIMS-AUDIT.md` |
| **Difficulty** (1-5) | Operational: skill, consistency, ticket time, hold quality. 5 = hardest to run nightly |
| **Equipment** | What the dish cannot be made without. Hoods, fuel, gas/charcoal and code all `[PRO]` |
| **Cost risk** | L/M/H = how exposed the plate is to protein price swings, waste, yield and labour. **No dollar figures exist**; cost cards come at test stage 3 |
| **Test status** | T0 desk concept (all dishes today) → T1 test kitchen → T2 blind panel → T3 pop-up/guest data → T4 costed recipe card + speed-of-service trial → LAUNCH. Kill criteria stated where a dish is conditional |
| **Heat** | 1-3 scale from `SAUCE-SYSTEM.md` |

## 5. Menu logic checks (applied to all 16)
1. **Cooking-logic reason exists** (shared technique, structure or real crossing): passes for all but 13 (conditional).
2. **Shared-ingredient load:** thigh, paneer, dough/tortilla, corn masa, poblano/tomato/onion/cilantro/lime/curry leaf/ginger-garlic appear in most dishes.
3. **Ticket time:** every dish ≤ 8-10 minutes from fire except dum biryani (batch, 25-40 min dum; hold) and birria (pre-braised).
4. **Beer logic:** at least three pairing partners from the beer list per dish exist (see beer program section 5).
5. **Allergen and diet:** every item has a heat level and an allergen note.
6. **No novelty fusion:** dish 13 is the only dish that fails the "needs to exist" test until proved.

## 7. Test plan (concept to launch)
| Stage | What | Pass criteria | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 Test kitchen** | Cook each dish 3 iterations; record yields, times, hold, sauce stability | Edible, consistent, hold ≥ 10 min (fried) or 2 h (braises) | Draft recipe card |
| **T2 Blind panel** | ≥ 8 tasters incl. at least 2 chefs of each cuisine background **[PRO/advisors]**; score identity ("is this recognisable to X?"), balance, craveability; pair with beers | Average ≥ 4/5 on identity and balance; Birria Pizza and Mushroom-Methi must beat kill criteria | Keep / rework / cut |
| **T3 Pop-up** | 2-4 events, 6-10 dishes, 30+ customer interviews; record sell-through, ticket time, waste | Top-selling dishes plus vegetarian share; waste < threshold set at T1 | Menu v2; price ranges |
| **T4 Costed recipe cards + speed-of-service** | Weighed recipes, yield, supplier quotes, labour time | Cost and time within model assumptions from `business/AUSTIN-FEASIBILITY-V1.md` | Launch menu |
