# Founder Photography Protocol for the First Real Cook (Chicken 65 Totopos, Paneer Tikka Taco)

**Status:** `[PLAN]` documentation protocol. Goal: **evidence first, marketing later.** A modern smartphone and a window are enough. These photographs are the only thing that can replace a concept render, and only through `food/t1-test-sheets/09-concept-vs-real-comparison.md`.

## Setup (free)
Daylight from a side window, no flash, no filters, no editing. Plain dark or wooden surface, the real service vessel (black stoneware), a ruler or a 10 cm marker and a scale in at least one shot. Wipe the lens. Lock focus and exposure by tapping the food. Shoot level horizontally; keep the phone parallel to the table for top-down.

## Shots (name files `DISH-CONSTRUCTION-SHOT-TIME`, for example `C65-A-hero-0min.jpg`)
1. **Ingredients and labels**: every packaging label (lard, rennet, stock, gelatin statements) and raw weights on the scale.
2. **Hero (45 degrees)**: the plate as served, full plate in frame, same height as a seated guest's eyes.
3. **Truth (top-down)**: directly above, whole plate, nothing hidden; this is the honest view.
4. **Construction (close-up)**: a chip with chicken, a cut paneer cube, the tortilla fold; show the interior (break one piece open).
5. **Hold test**: the same plate at **0, 5 and 10 minutes**, same position and light, with a probe thermometer reading in frame.
6. **Portion**: the full serving beside the scale.
7. **Failure**: photograph soggy chips, dry chicken, torn tortilla honestly. Failures are evidence.
8. **Beer**: if a beer is tried with it, the glass beside the plate (any beer; do not claim it is a NASHA beer).

## Rules
Record cook date, who cooked, quantities and construction on the test sheet the same day. Never stage, restyle or edit to look better than it was. Keep originals. Do not post photos publicly until rights and the test record exist. People in the frame need their permission.
