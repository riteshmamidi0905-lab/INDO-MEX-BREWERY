# House Sauce System (8)

> NOTE: "Raita-Crema" is a house label only. Use "crema" per research/CORE5-PRODUCT-SYSTEM.md.

Principle: each sauce exists because a **cooking structure** is shared across both cuisines, not because chiles + tamarind sounds fun. All formulas are `[TEST]`; this is architecture, not recipe.

| # | Sauce | Structure (what it is) | Why it exists | Core components `[C]` | Heat | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lal** (red chile paste) | Dried-red-chile paste, sour + garlic | Indian red-chile pastes (e.g., Telangana/Andhra) and Mexican guajillo adobos are both dried-chile-forward bases | Dried red chiles (Indian + guajillo blend), garlic, tamarind, salt, oil | 2-3 | 65 dust/marinade, wings, birria adobo cross-base, pizza base |
| 2 | **Hari** (green chutney-salsa) | Raw green herb sauce | Mint-cilantro chutney and salsa verde cruda are both raw, acidic herb-chile sauces | Cilantro, mint, serrano/green chile, lime, tomatillo or yogurt (two versions) | 1-2 | Tacos, 65 totopos, tikka, pizza |
| 3 | **Imli-Guajillo glaze** | Sweet-sour-chile glaze | Tamarind chutney and tamarind-chile candy/sauces occupy the same sweet-sour-heat slot (tamarind is used in both cuisines `[V S-47a,b]`) | Tamarind, guajillo, jaggery/piloncillo, cumin | 1-2 | Gobi, wings, dessert drizzle |
| 4 | **Malabar** (pepper-coconut) | Aromatic coconut-pepper base | Kerala fish marinades and coastal Mexican fish need acid + fat + aromatics | Black pepper, coconut, curry leaf, ginger, lime | 2 | Fish taco, wings, crema |
| 5 | **Makhani-Rajas** | Tomato-butter-cream, smoked | Butter chicken's sauce lacks smoke/acid; roasted poblano (rajas) gives both | Roasted tomato, roasted poblano, butter, cream, kasuri methi | 1 | Quesadilla, pizza, biryani side |
| 6 | **Adobo-Dum** (birria base + consommé) | Chile adobo with sealed braise | Birria adobo + dum technique share low-and-slow braise logic (Telangana × Jalisco, 6.9) | Guajillo/ancho/pasilla, vinegar, whole spices (low), yogurt-papaya pre-marinade (Indian) | 2 | Goat birria (goat only; beef option removed, D-54), birria pizza (conditional) |
| 7 | **Macha-Tadka** (crunch oil) | Chile-nut-seed oil | Salsa macha (chile, nuts, seeds in oil) and a South-Indian tadka with peanuts/sesame/curry leaf are the same form: crunchy infused oil | Chile de árbol, peanut, sesame, mustard seed, curry leaf, oil | 2-3 | Eggplant tostada, dal-frijol, eggs, finishing |
| 8 | **Raita-Crema** (cooling) | Cultured dairy cooler | Raita and crema/sour cream do the same cooling job against heat | Yogurt + crema, roasted garlic, lime, cumin, mint | 0 | Everything hot; kids' dips |

## Pickles and garnish (not sauces)
- **Achari Onion:** pickled red onion with mustard seed, fennel, nigella, lime (Indian pickle logic + Mexican cebolla en vinagre).
- **Kokum-lime:** ceviche-style cure for fish (tiny dose; test).

## Heat architecture
Level 1 (mild): Raita-Crema, Makhani-Rajas, Hari (yogurt) · Level 2: Malabar, Adobo-Dum, Imli-Guajillo · Level 3: Lal, Macha-Tadka. Each dish lists heat and the cooling sauce.

## Prep overlap
Sauces 1, 3, 6 share dried-chile rehydration and blending; 2 and 4 share fresh herbs/aromatics; 5 and 8 share dairy station. Estimated prep: 3 stations. `[Unresolved]`
