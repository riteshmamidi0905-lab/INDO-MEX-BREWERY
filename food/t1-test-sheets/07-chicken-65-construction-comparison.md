**Primary direction = INTEGRATED**: Chicken 65 distributed through the totopos so the plate reads as one dish (Chicken 65 x Mexican totopos), not nachos with red chicken and not Chicken 65 beside chips. **Integrated is a direction, not a finding.** The kitchen test decides which construction performs best; the concept image never dictates the product.
