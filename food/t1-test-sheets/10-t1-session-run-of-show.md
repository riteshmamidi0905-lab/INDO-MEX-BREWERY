## Run of show (times are relative; adjust to the kitchen)
1. **Prep (T-90 to T-0):** marinate and prepare chicken per the chosen method; prepare totopos (one batch, divided across A, B, C); lime, onion, curry leaf, green chile; optional crema as a side. Weigh everything; photograph labels.
2. **Cook batch 1:** fry chicken; fry/toast chips. Plate **A, B, C from the same batches** with the same chicken weight and chip weight per plate. Record weights.
3. **Stagger:** start each construction 3 minutes apart with a stopwatch per plate, log room temperature, and say whether holds are ambient or under a lamp (default: **table conditions, ambient**). **At plating (T+0)** (score within 60 seconds): probe temperature; photograph each construction: hero (45 degrees), truth (top-down), construction close-up, with the label hidden. Taste panel scores T+0.
4. **T+5 and T+10:** leave plates untouched (score crispness from **extra chips plated on the side for each time point**, not from the bitten plate; the bare-chip control is scored the same way); photograph and probe at each time; score crispness, sauce migration, structure, flavour again.
5. **Failures:** photograph and record honestly (soggy chips, dry chicken, broken chips). Do not rearrange.
6. **Repeat** at least once with a fresh batch to test repeatability (iteration 2).
7. **Paneer Tikka Taco: run in a SECOND session** (one cook cannot do both dishes well); cook per sheet 03; plate two tacos; score paneer identity, char, marinade, moisture, tortilla compatibility, garnish, acid, heat, structure, Indian, Mexican and NASHA identity; hold at 0/5/10; photograph hero/truth/construction/failure.
8. **Beer (optional):** if any beer is tried with a dish, record which and what it did (not a NASHA beer; no claims).
