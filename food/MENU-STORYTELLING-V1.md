# Menu Storytelling System v1

**Status:** `[PROPOSED]` guest-facing language for the **working test menu** (`MENU-V1.1-TEST-MENU.md`; the menu is not changed here). **No dish has been cooked or tasted; every description says what the dish is *designed* to be.** Policy: **no beef, no pork** (D-54); **not halal**. Cultural classification: `brand/CULTURAL-INTEGRITY-AND-ADVISORS.md`. Pairings: `beer/OPENING-BEER-PROGRAM-V1.md`. Visuals: IDs in `visuals/ASSET-MANIFEST.md` / `VISUAL-WORLD-AND-BRIEFS-V1.md`. Final wording needs T1/T2 results, advisor review and allergen verification.

## 1. Writing rules
1. **Plain verbs, one line (≤ 18 words).** Use "designed around", "intended to combine", "inspired by" until tested.
2. **Name the inspiration without claiming origin.** "Our own dish" for original compositions.
3. **Real dish names stay real** (Chicken 65, birria); borrowed regional names only if the dish meets the tradition's identity test, else "-style".
4. **Heat** 0-3 shown as chiles; the cooling element is named.
5. **Dietary markers (provisional):** V = designed vegetarian (strict status pending cheese-rennet, stock, gelatin verification), D = dairy, G = gluten (wheat tortilla/dough), N/S = peanut or sesame possible, F = fish. A global line: "**We do not serve beef or pork.**" (only after kitchen verification; D-54). **No halal, vegan or gluten-free claim** without verification.
6. Allergen markers are **preliminary** and must be verified recipe by recipe.

## 4. Menu format concept **[Proposed]**
One page, five sections, a heat scale, a short "our own dish / inspired by" line, and a **pairing strip** (beer name + one reason). A back-of-card "How to eat it" for birria/tadka moments. Digital menu with dietary filters; printed card on black-stoneware-toned stock.

## 5. What this does **not** establish
Flavour, texture, cost, speed or guest appeal. Those require T1-T4 and customer evidence.
