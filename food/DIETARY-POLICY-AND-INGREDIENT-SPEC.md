# Dietary Policy and Ingredient Specification (D-54)

**Policy (founder, binding):** **No beef. No pork.** Primary proteins: **chicken, goat, lamb, fish/seafood, paneer, cauliflower, mushrooms, lentils, beans, vegetables.** Vegetarian food is a major pillar, not an accommodation. Applies to every menu surface: permanent menu, specials, kids menu, sauces, stocks, broths, fats, toppings, events, collaborations, supplier assumptions, costing and guest-facing text. Reversal only by the founder.
**This is not a halal claim.** "No beef/no pork" ≠ halal. Do not use "halal" on any surface until sourcing, slaughter certification, ingredient certification, cross-contact, kitchen operations, alcohol-related considerations and community expectations have been reviewed `[PRO]`.

## 1. Hidden-derivative checklist (supplier and recipe screening) `[TEST]`
| Risk | Typical hiding place | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Lard / pork or beef fat | Flour tortillas, refried beans, churros, pastry, pie crust, fried-food oil, some "vegetable shortening" | Specify vegetable oil or ghee; get written supplier statements |
| Beef/pork stock, bouillon, bases | Soups, rice, moles, birria consommé, gravies | Chicken/goat/vegetable stock only; no commercial bases without spec sheets |
| Gelatin | Desserts, marshmallow, gummy items, some sauces, clarified drinks | Plant-based or none; never beef/pork gelatin |
| Animal rennet | Cheeses (cotija-style, Oaxaca-style, paneer is acid-set) | Ask for microbial/vegetarian rennet or accept only suppliers who state source; label policy for vegetarian guests is separate |
| Bacon/pork in garnishes (prohibited, D-54) | Elote toppings, beans, pizza toppings | None |
| Worcestershire, some flavour bases, "natural flavors" | Marinades, sauces | Spec sheets |
| Shared equipment | Supplier plants, co-packers | Ask suppliers about co-processing |
| Beer fining and finishing | Gelatin/isinglass fining | Brewery uses **no gelatin** (isinglass is fish-derived: a vegetarian-labelling question, decide with the head brewer) |
| Collaboration, guest and event food | Third-party caterers | Same policy in contracts |

## 2. Vocabulary rule for guest-facing text
Plain statement allowed after kitchen verification: **"We do not serve beef or pork."** Do not add halal, kosher, "clean", "ethically sourced" or similar claims without evidence. Describe goat and lamb plainly.

## 4. Impact on the concept

- **Flour tortillas** must be made or bought **lard-free**.
