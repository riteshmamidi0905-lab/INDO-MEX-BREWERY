## Chicken Tikka Nachos (working label)
Concept: dry-charred yogurt-marinated chicken tikka on fried corn chips, hari salsa, pickled onion, lime. Heritage: chicken tikka is Punjabi/North Indian; nachos are Mexican-American (Piedras Negras, 1940s); the combination is MODERN FUSION. Ingredients (policy-consistent): chicken thigh, hung curd, ginger-garlic, Kashmiri chile, kasuri methi, lemon, neutral oil (no mustard oil, V-72), fried nixtamal chips, optional Oaxaca/Chihuahua-style cheese, hari salsa, cilantro, lime. Risks: chip sogginess in ~10 min, cheese hold, dry chicken, dairy, shared fryer, identity (reads as generic nachos). Workflow hypothesis: marinate 4-8 h; char, rest, chop; fry chips; build chips > chicken > cheese under salamander > cold parts; compare cheese-free. Pairing hypotheses: PAAPI (clove/banana vs char, carbonation vs dairy); BADSHAH possibly too sweet against hot salsa. Test design: crispness 1-5 at T+0/5/10, juiciness, probe 74 C, build time, three-yes test (tikka, Mexican, not generic), bagged-chip control, n=2.

## Nawaabi Enchiladas (working label)
Concept: corn tortillas with tikka-style chicken or paneer, silky Mughlai-style gravy with whole spices, baked briefly, pickled onion, cilantro, chile oil. Heritage: Mughlai (court cuisine: cream, nut paste, whole spices) and enchiladas (Mexican) are separate; combining is MODERN FUSION; "Mughlai/Nawaabi" needs advisor review. Cashew-free default (tree nuts, poppy seed, almond are allergens). Risks: nut allergen, dairy, soggy/torn tortillas, split gravy on hold, tastes like butter chicken, gluten if flour tortillas. Test design: gravy stability after 30 min hot hold, tears per roll, time to sogginess, blind "distinct from butter chicken" score, nut-free vs nut-paste. Pairing hypotheses: PAAPI (softness vs cardamom); BADSHAH likely too bitter vs cream.
