# INDO MEX BREWERY: Master Concept (v1)

**Status:** `[FOUNDER VISION]` + `[PROPOSED]`. This is the canonical concept statement; it consolidates, and does not replace, the detailed documents it links to. **"INDO MEX BREWERY" is the working project name, not a brand decision** (D-11, D-12). Nothing here has been customer-validated, brewed, cooked, quoted, legally reviewed or architect-reviewed. Evidence labels used throughout: **[Vision]** founder/design intent · **[Evidence]** something actually in the repository with its tier (`research/VERIFICATION-LEDGER.md`) · **[Needs validation]**.

## 1. The concept in one paragraph
A destination brewery-restaurant in Austin where **the beer is made in plain sight and the kitchen is designed around it**: wheat beers and lagers brewed to meet the heat, acid, fat and smoke of Indian- and Mexican-inspired cooking, served in a shaded, family-welcoming place built for Texas weather. The cultural combination must be **earned through flavour, technique and hospitality, not decoration**. Concept visual authority: ARC-01-D (day exterior, `visuals/generated/ARC-01-D.png`); working menu `food/MENU-V1.1-TEST-MENU.md`; beer `beer/OPENING-BEER-PROGRAM-V1.md`; space `architecture/MASTER-PLAN-PHASE2.md`.

## 2. The deeper proposition (not "Indian food + Mexican food + beer")
**Food and beer conceived together, from fermenter to table.** Most breweries add food to beer, or add beer to a food concept. The proposition is a **pairing-led brewpub**: every beer has a job at the table, every dish is designed with a beer in mind, and the guest can *taste why* a pairing works (carbonation against fat, low bitterness beside chile, toast against char). India and Mexico are the **culinary vocabulary** because both cooking traditions are built on heat, acid, fat, toasted spice/seed/nut and ferment/pickle logic, which makes them unusually good partners for clean wheat beers and lagers. That similarity is **a cooking-logic analogy, not a shared history** (`research/HISTORICAL-CLAIMS-AUDIT.md` addendum G). **[Vision; needs validation]**

## 3. Customer promise **[Vision]**
"Come to a place where you can watch your beer being made, eat food you have not quite had before but that makes sense, learn why they go together without being lectured, and stay a while in the shade." Specifically guests should get: (1) real, visible brewing; (2) a menu that explains itself through pairing; (3) approachable classic beers done well; (4) a comfortable, shaded place for groups and families; (5) honesty about what is tradition, what is our own composition.

## 4. Positioning **[Proposed; needs validation]**
- **Category:** culinary-led destination brewpub (pairing-led), at brewpub scale, not a production brewery with a food truck.
- **Against:** Austin IPA-led production breweries, taco-and-beer neighbourhood spots, German beer halls, farm breweries, patio-and-string-light taprooms (`research/AUSTIN-COMPETITIVE-LANDSCAPE.md`, T3 + site summaries).
- **Differentiators (hypotheses):** pairing system; wheat-and-lager-first beer; vegetarian-strong, no-beef/no-pork kitchen (D-54); visible brewing as architecture; climate-engineered shade; one coherent design language.
- **Not:** a generic Indian restaurant, a Mexican cantina, a sports bar or a themed "fusion" venue.

## 8. Why Austin / why India × Mexico / why craft beer: honest versions
- **Why Austin [Vision + T3]:** a food-and-beer city with a consolidating brewery market (differentiate or lose) and a climate that rewards shade-engineered gathering places. *Gap:* demand data.
- **Why India × Mexico [Vision]:** chosen for cooking-logic compatibility and the beer-pairing opportunity, plus a documented, specific diaspora (Punjabi-Mexican families in California, verified by scholarship, `HISTORICAL-CLAIMS-AUDIT.md`). **No personal founder origin story is claimed (D-09).** Maize/millet, tandoor/comal, mole/salan are analogies only.
- **Why craft beer [Vision]:** beer is the connective tissue for the pairing system and the visible craft. *Risk:* Austin demand skews hop-forward (T3).
